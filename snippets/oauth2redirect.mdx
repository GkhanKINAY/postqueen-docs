The redirect URI is where the network sends the browser back after someone approves PostQueen. It is
your `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/` + the network's identifier, for example
`https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/pinterest`.

Most networks need exactly one URI, with no trailing slash. Two exceptions: TikTok Business needs
the trailing slash (`/integrations/social/tiktok-business/`), and a Google web client that also
serves Google Business Profile lists both `/integrations/social/youtube` and
`/integrations/social/gmb`. [Channel developer apps](/installation/providers/overview) lists every
network's ending.
