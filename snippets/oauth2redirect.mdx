The OAuth2 Redirect URI is the location where the provider will redirect to after a successful login. This needs to be set to your PostQueen `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/` + `{provider}`.

Most networks need exactly one URI. Google is the exception: sign-in, YouTube and Google My Business can share one web client, and each path (`/youtube`, `/gmb`) has to be listed. Never add a trailing slash.
