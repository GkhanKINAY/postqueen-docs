Restart PostQueen so the new variables are read. With Docker Compose, run `docker compose down`
and then `docker compose up -d`; a plain `restart` reuses the old container and its old
environment.

Then open the web interface, click **Add Channel** in the left sidebar and pick this network. You
are sent to its login to authorize PostQueen, and the channel appears in the sidebar once you
accept.

<Check>
The channel is in the left sidebar with its avatar and name. That is the whole confirmation: if it
is in the list, she can post to it.
</Check>

<Note>
**Add Channel does not check whether your variables are set.** A missing or misspelled key shows
up as a broken authorization page on the network's side rather than a helpful message in
PostQueen, so if the login page itself looks wrong, check the keys before anything else.
</Note>
