## What you can post

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Text" icon="type">
    Up to 63,206 characters.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Media" icon="image">
    Photos. MP4 video is left out of the post.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Post types" icon="layers">
    Timeline post, or a post to one group you belong to.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Analytics" icon="chart-line">
    No analytics for MeWe.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Not supported:** video, MeWe pages, several groups in one post, and comments or follow-ups.

## Connect MeWe

<Note>
MeWe is listed in **Add Channel**, but it cannot be connected on PostQueen yet.
</Note>

## Post settings

Each MeWe post in the composer has these settings:

- **Post To**: **My Timeline** or **Group**. It starts on **My Timeline**.
- **Select Group**: the group to post to, for a **Group** post. Required there: a group post without one is refused with "Select a group to post to".

The field names and values for the API are on [MeWe posting settings](/public-api/providers/mewe).

## Good to know

- Only MP4 files are left out. Any other file, a MOV video included, is sent to MeWe as a photo, and MeWe may refuse it.
- The link to a published post opens your timeline or the group, not the post itself.
- The connection lasts until MeWe's sign-in expires, or 30 days, and cannot be renewed on its own. Reconnect the channel when it asks.
