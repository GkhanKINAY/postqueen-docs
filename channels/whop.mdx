PostQueen will post Markdown forum posts, with an optional title, images or video, and threaded replies, to a forum in a Whop company you have access to.

## What you can post

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Text" icon="type">
    Up to 50,000 characters, sent as Markdown.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Media" icon="image">
    Images or video, uploaded to Whop and attached to the post.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Post types" icon="layers">
    A forum post, with or without a title, and threaded replies.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Analytics" icon="chart-line">
    No analytics for Whop.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Not supported:** posts outside a forum.

## Connect Whop

<Note>
Whop is listed in **Add Channel**, but it cannot be connected on PostQueen yet. Connecting will ask you to sign in to Whop and approve access.
</Note>

## Post settings

Each Whop post in the composer has these settings:

- **Select Company**: the company whose forums are listed. Required.
- **Select Forum**: the forum to post in. Required.
- **Title (optional)**: the post's title. Left empty, the post has none.

The field names and values for the API are on [Whop posting settings](/public-api/providers/whop).

## Good to know

- Before a post is scheduled, PostQueen checks that it is at most 50,000 characters and that a company and a forum are picked.
- **Add comment** adds replies, each with its own delay and its own attachments. The first replies to the post, and each later one replies to the one before it.
- Every file is uploaded to Whop before the post is created. If Whop has not finished processing a file after about 9 minutes, the post fails with "File upload timed out".
- The company and forum lists show the first 50 of each.
- A post to a forum that was deleted, or that you no longer have access to, fails with "Forum or experience not found".
