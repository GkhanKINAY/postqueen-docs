## What she can post to TikTok

TikTok has the deepest settings panel of any network PostQueen posts to. Privacy, comments and the duet or stitch toggles travel with the post, as do the branded-content and AI labels. Until TikTok's audit of PostQueen's app passes, every post publishes as Self only, whatever privacy level you pick.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Character limit" icon="type">
    2,000 characters. Photo post titles cap at 90
  </Card>
  <Card title="Media" icon="images">
    Always required. One video, or one or more pictures
  </Card>
  <Card title="Post types" icon="layers">
    Video post, photo post
  </Card>
  <Card title="Analytics" icon="chart-line">
    Yes, for the account and for each post
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Account numbers come back as followers, following and total likes, plus video count and recent engagement. Per post she reads views and likes, plus comments and shares.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Post settings** | `content_posting_method`, `privacy_level`, `title`, `duet`, `stitch`, `comment`, `autoAddMusic`, `brand_content_toggle`, `brand_organic_toggle`, `video_made_with_ai`. Full schema in the [TikTok settings reference](/public-api/providers/tiktok) |
| **Connect flow** | One step. You authorize the account and she attaches it |
| **Automations** | None |

<Warning>
TikTok is strict about what it accepts:

- **No video / images selected.** Every TikTok post needs an attachment.
- **Only pictures are supported when selecting multiple items.** Videos go one at a time.
- **Video must be at least 720p, Picture must no exceed 1080p.**
- **TikTok limits pending posts to 5 within any 24-hour period.** Those are posts waiting in your TikTok inbox, so clear them before sending more.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**Known issue:** the `/integrations/social/tiktok` connect page can crash for some accounts. If you see a blank page or "cannot read properties of undefined" error, retry from a fresh incognito window. Tracked in [Known Issues](/troubleshooting/known-issues).
</Warning>

## Posting settings

When composing a TikTok post, the "How do you want to post?" setting decides which other settings apply:

- **Post directly to TikTok** publishes the post and applies all the settings below.
- **Upload without posting** does **not** publish: it sends the media to the account's TikTok app inbox, where the owner must finish and publish it manually within 24 hours or it is discarded. TikTok keeps only the post content/title in this mode, so the settings panel collapses to hide the other settings while it is selected.

Some settings also depend on the media type:

- **Allow Duet**, **Allow Stitch**, and **Video made with AI** apply to videos only, TikTok has no equivalent for photo posts.
- **Auto add music** applies to photos only.
- Privacy level, comments, and the branded-content toggles apply to both videos and photos.

## TikTok Business

<Warning>
**Soon.** The TikTok Business card is listed in Add Channel, but it cannot be connected on PostQueen yet.
</Warning>

Add Channel can list two TikTok cards: **TikTok** and **TikTok Business**. The second connects the account through TikTok's Business API, and the difference shows up mostly in what a post can carry.

| | TikTok | TikTok Business |
|---|---|---|
| **Caption** | 2,000 characters | 2,200 characters |
| **Pictures per post** | One or more | Up to 35 |
| **Music** | **Auto add music** lets TikTok choose, photos only | Pick a track from TikTok's commercial music list, for videos and photos. **Auto add music** on a photo picks one at random |
| **Location tag** | No | Yes |
| **Privacy level** | Videos and photos | Photos only. A video publishes with the account's default visibility |
| **Analytics** | Account and each post | Account and each post |

Music and the location tag apply only to **Post directly to TikTok**, like the other settings. Over the API they are the `music` and `location` fields in the [TikTok settings reference](/public-api/providers/tiktok), found with the `musicSearch` and `locationSearch` [provider tools](/public-api/integrations/provider-tools).
