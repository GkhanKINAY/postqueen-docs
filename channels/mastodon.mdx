PostQueen will post public Mastodon posts and threads with images, video and alt text, and wait for your server to finish processing a video before it posts.

## What you can post

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Text" icon="type">
    Up to 500 characters, even on a server that allows more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Media" icon="image">
    Images and MP4 video, with alt text. How many a post can carry is your server's rule, usually 4 images or 1 video.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Post types" icon="layers">
    Post and thread. Every post is public.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Analytics" icon="chart-line">
    No analytics for Mastodon.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Not supported:** unlisted, followers-only and direct posts, content warnings, polls and quote posts.

## Connect Mastodon

<Note>
Mastodon is listed in **Add Channel**, but it cannot be connected on PostQueen yet. It will connect accounts on one Mastodon server: accounts on other servers cannot be connected.
</Note>

## Post settings

Mastodon posts have no settings of their own in the composer: the text, the media and each file's alt text are all a post carries. The API fields are on [Mastodon posting settings](/public-api/providers/mastodon).

## Good to know

- **Continue thread** adds parts to the post. Each part is posted as a reply to the part before, can carry its own media and has its own delay.
- PostQueen waits until your server has processed a video before it creates the post. Media in later thread parts is posted without that wait, so a part with a large video can fail.
- The number of files per post is not checked first: your server refuses a post that carries more than it allows.
- The first post carries a key that stops Mastodon from publishing it twice when the request is retried.
