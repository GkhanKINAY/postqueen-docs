## Just talk to her

Message her the way you would message a colleague. She answers inside ChatGPT and the Claude app, reads your WhatsApp and Telegram messages through OpenClaw or Hermes, and she is one command away in your terminal. Tell her what you want the world to see, and she takes care of the rest: the words, the visual and the schedule.

<img src="/images/brand/open-agents.svg" alt="Chat apps feed OpenClaw, a one-line brief feeds Hermes, both drive the postqueen CLI and posts land on the calendar" />

## She runs on your terms

Leave her alone and she is a true autopilot: whatever lands on the calendar goes out on time without another word from you. Want a closer look first? Everything is visible on the calendar before it publishes, so you can edit or delete it, and a post created as a draft is saved but never scheduled, so it sits there until you publish it.

## Install the skill

One command installs her skill into any coding agent that supports the `skills` registry:

```bash
npx skills add GkhanKINAY/postqueen-agent
```

What lands is her `SKILL.md`, a playbook that teaches your agent how she works before it runs a
single command. After reading it, the agent knows how to find your channels, ask each platform
what it accepts, fetch things that cannot be hardcoded like subreddit flairs, and upload media
the way she requires.

<Card title="Install the skill, step by step" icon="download" href="/agents/skill-install">
  The API key, the CLI, self-hosted setup, and a table of what goes wrong with each fix
</Card>

Every agent session then runs the same loop: authenticate, find your channels, prepare media,
post, check the numbers. All seven steps with the command each one runs:
[Why agents love her](/agents/why-agents).

## Connect over MCP

Prefer tool calls over shell commands? PostQueen speaks MCP at one URL she hosts, so an MCP client gets 11 tools to list your channels, upload media and schedule posts without installing anything.

There is one URL and every client takes it a slightly different way: a single command in Claude
Code, one JSON entry in Cursor, a connector form on claude.ai, a bridge in Claude Desktop.

## From your chat apps

Message her on WhatsApp or Telegram through OpenClaw or Hermes, and the same route brings her into Slack and Discord. Prefer the browser? [app.postqueen.ai](https://app.postqueen.ai) runs your whole calendar from your phone too. Setup: [OpenClaw](/agents/openclaw) and [Hermes](/agents/hermes).

## Try saying

Once she is connected, talk to her in plain words:

> *"Plan a month of content for our coffee shop and fill the calendar."*

> *"Take this photo of today's special and put it on Instagram at lunchtime."*

> *"We just hit 10k followers, write a warm thank-you post for all our channels."*

> *"Turn my latest YouTube video into posts for X, LinkedIn and Threads."*

She turns any of those into scheduled posts across your channels, and the calendar stays yours to review.
