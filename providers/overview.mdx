30+ networks, one calendar. She posts to all of them, and each one connects on its own terms: an OAuth login for most, a credential form for Bluesky and WordPress, the browser extension for Skool.

Nothing is connected by default, and every network is added the same way, from **Add Channel**. What changes is whether the network sends you to its own login or asks you to paste a key in the form.

<Info>
On PostQueen Cloud the OAuth apps are already registered. A network missing from **Add Channel** is a [self-hosted](/installation/overview) concern: each platform needs an application you create yourself, then a restart. Walkthroughs live under [Connect your channels](/installation/providers/instagram).
</Info>

A smaller group takes the credential in the connect form, so you paste an API key or a login there and you are done. The card for each one below says which kind it is.

## Analytics reaches ten of them

Posting is the wide part of PostQueen. Measuring is the narrow one. Ten networks report numbers back to her per channel, and nine of those also per post. Google My Business is the exception: it reports at the location level and has nothing per post. Every other platform publishes without a scoreboard. So an empty analytics view on Reddit or Telegram is the platform being quiet, not your setup being broken.

- Facebook Page
- Google My Business
- Instagram (Facebook Business)
- Instagram (Standalone)
- LinkedIn Page
- Pinterest
- Threads
- TikTok
- X (Twitter)
- YouTube

Two things catch people out. LinkedIn personal profiles are not on that list, only LinkedIn Pages report back. And Google My Business answers only for verified profiles inside the date range it allows, so it returns an empty payload rather than an error.

One more, on purpose: set `DISABLE_X_ANALYTICS` and she stops asking X for numbers at all, which is what you want when your X plan does not include analytics access. See [X (Twitter)](/providers/x-twitter).
