30+ networks, one calendar. She posts to all of them, and each one connects on its own terms: OAuth keys for most, a plain account login for Bluesky, the browser extension for Skool.

Nothing is connected by default, and every network is added the same way, from Add Channel. What changes is where the credential goes.

Most networks want an OAuth app you register with the platform. Those keys go in your `.env` file, or into the environment some other way, and PostQueen reads them at boot. Change one and restart her before you expect it to take effect. Running docker compose? Run `docker compose down` and then `docker compose up` to rebuild the containers with the updated variables.

A smaller group asks for nothing in your `.env` file at all. Those take their credential straight in the connect form, so you paste an API key or a login there and you are done. Nothing to restart, nothing to redeploy. The card for each one below says which kind it is, and the [Configuration Reference](/configuration/reference) lists the ones that need no variable.

## Analytics reaches ten of them

Posting is the wide part of PostQueen. Measuring is the narrow one. Ten networks report numbers back to her, per channel and per post, and every other platform publishes without a scoreboard. So an empty analytics view on Reddit or Telegram is the platform being quiet, not your setup being broken.

- Facebook Page
- Google My Business
- Instagram (Facebook Business)
- Instagram (Standalone)
- LinkedIn Page
- Pinterest
- Threads
- TikTok
- X (Twitter)
- YouTube

Two things catch people out. LinkedIn personal profiles are not on that list, only LinkedIn Pages report back. And Google My Business answers only for verified profiles inside the date range it allows, so it returns an empty payload rather than an error.

One more, on purpose: set `DISABLE_X_ANALYTICS` and she stops asking X for numbers at all, which is what you want when your X plan does not include analytics access. See [X (Twitter)](/providers/x-twitter).
