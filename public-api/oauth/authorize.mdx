---
title: "Check an authorization request"
description: "Check a Client ID and get the app name and picture PostQueen shows on its consent screen."
openapi: "GET /oauth/authorize"
---

You do not need this call to run the OAuth flow. Send people to `https://app.postqueen.ai/oauth/authorize` with your `client_id`, `response_type=code` and `state`: that page makes this call, shows your app's name, description and picture, and asks the person to **Authorize** or **Deny**. The whole flow is on [OAuth apps](/public-api/oauth).

It is useful to check a Client ID from your own code: a valid one answers `200` with the app's details, and an unknown one `400 Invalid client_id`.

<Note>
This endpoint is outside `/public/v1` and takes no API key. A client that registered itself must also send one of its `redirect_uris`, and one without a secret must send a PKCE `code_challenge` with method `S256`.
</Note>
