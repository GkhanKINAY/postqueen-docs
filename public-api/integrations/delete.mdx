---
title: "Delete a channel"
sidebarTitle: "Delete a channel"
description: "Remove a channel together with every post it has in PostQueen."
openapi: "DELETE /integrations/{id}"
---

<Warning>
Deleting a channel deletes all of its posts in PostQueen first: scheduled posts, drafts, published records and failed posts. Pending publishing is cancelled. Connecting the same account again does not bring them back.
</Warning>

What the delete does not do:

- It does not touch the same content scheduled on your other channels. Each channel has its own posts.
- It does not take anything down from the network. Published posts stay live there.

An id that is not a channel of your workspace answers `500`, not `404`. Check it against [List channels](/public-api/integrations/list) first.
