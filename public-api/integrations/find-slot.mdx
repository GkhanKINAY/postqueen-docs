---
title: "Find a free slot"
description: "The next posting time on a channel that has no post yet."
openapi: "GET /find-slot/{id}"
---

The slots come from the channel's [posting times](/using/posting-times). The answer has no time zone suffix but is UTC. You can send it back as a post's `date` as it is: PostQueen reads a date without a zone as UTC.
