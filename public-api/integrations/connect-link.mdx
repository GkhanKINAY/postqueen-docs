---
title: "Create a connect link"
description: "A PostQueen page that connects one new channel to your workspace, for someone to open in a browser."
openapi: "POST /integrations/connect"
---

[Get a connect URL](/public-api/integrations/connect) returns the network's own sign-in page. This returns a PostQueen page instead: whoever opens it first reads what connecting the channel means, then continues to the network to sign in and approve. They do not need a PostQueen account, so you can send the link to a client or a teammate.

```json
{ "integration": "linkedin-page", "redirectUrl": "https://example.com/settings/social" }
```

## How long a link works

- One hour, and one channel. Once a channel is connected through it, create a new link for the next one.
- A sign-in that was cancelled on the network can be tried again with the same link.
- It cannot reconnect a channel you already have. Use [Get a connect URL](/public-api/integrations/connect) with `refresh` for that.

## Which networks it is for

The networks that sign in on their own consent page: Instagram (`instagram`, `instagram-standalone`), Facebook, Threads, X, TikTok, TikTok Business, YouTube, LinkedIn, LinkedIn Page, Pinterest, Google Business Profile, Discord, Slack, Mastodon, Tumblr, Twitch, Kick, VK, Dribbble, MeWe and Whop. Any other network answers `400`: connect it from **Add Channel** in the app.

## After the sign-in

With a `redirectUrl`, the page names that website before the person continues, and the confirmation offers a button back to it. Its query string carries `added` (the network's identifier), `focus` (the new channel's id) and `msg` (a short status). Without one, a person signed in to PostQueen lands on their **Channels** page, and anyone else sees that the channel is connected.

<Check>
The new channel appears in [List channels](/public-api/integrations/list).
</Check>

<Note>
The plan's channel limit is checked when you create the link and again when the person continues. During the free trial, a network that unlocks when the trial ends answers `406`.
</Note>
