---
title: "List channels"
description: "Every channel connected to the workspace, with the id every other call takes."
openapi: "GET /integrations"
---

The `id` of each channel is what [Create a post](/public-api/posts/create), [Get a channel's settings](/public-api/integrations/settings), [Trigger a tool](/public-api/integrations/trigger) and the [analytics](/public-api/analytics/platform) endpoints take. `identifier` names the network, such as `bluesky` or `wordpress`; the settings each network takes are on [Posting settings by network](/public-api/providers/overview).

A channel with `disabled: true` is switched off in the app and does not publish until someone turns it back on.
