---
title: "Get a connect URL"
description: "The sign-in URL that starts connecting a network, for a person to open in a browser."
openapi: "GET /social/{integration}"
---

This returns a URL and nothing more. The network shows its own consent screen to a person, so a channel cannot be connected without someone clicking through it.

## Which networks it is for

It is for networks that sign in on their own consent page.

| Networks | What to do |
|---|---|
| Instagram (`instagram`, `instagram-standalone`), Facebook, Threads, X, TikTok, TikTok Business, YouTube, LinkedIn, LinkedIn Page, Pinterest, Reddit, Google Business Profile, Discord, Slack, Mastodon, Tumblr, Twitch, Kick, VK, Dribbble, MeWe, Whop | They sign in on their own consent page: open the `url` this returns |
| Bluesky, DEV, Hashnode, Lemmy, Listmonk, Moltbook, Nostr, WordPress | They connect with a handle, key or password: use **Add Channel** in the app. The `url` returned here is only an internal state value |
| Telegram, Farcaster, Skool | They connect through a bot code, an approval in the Farcaster app or the browser extension: use **Add Channel** in the app |

The `{integration}` value is the network's identifier, in lower case: `youtube`, `threads`, `instagram-standalone`. It is not always the display name.

## After the sign-in

The person lands back in the PostQueen app, which finishes the connection. The sign-in state lives for one hour, so generate a new URL for a link that sat unused longer than that.

<Check>
The new channel appears in [List channels](/public-api/integrations/list). Poll that, since this endpoint only hands out a URL.
</Check>

<Tip>
To reconnect a channel whose token expired, pass its id as `refresh`. PostQueen reconnects that channel instead of adding a new one, and a reconnect does not count against the plan's channel limit.
</Tip>
