---
title: "Change post status"
description: "Move a post between draft and scheduled without changing its content or date."
openapi: "PUT /posts/{id}/status"
---

Moving a draft to `schedule` runs the checks [Create a post](/public-api/posts/create) runs for a scheduled post, including the length and media rules a draft skipped. A draft that fails them answers 400 with the channel name and the reason, for example `Acme community: channel should not be null or undefined`, and stays a draft.

<Warning>
`schedule` keeps the post's stored date. If that date has already passed, the post publishes at once. Check the date in [List posts](/public-api/posts/list) first, and change it in the app when it is in the past.
</Warning>

A post that is already published, or failed, cannot change status. To publish a draft at a new time, change its date in the app, or send it again with [Create a post](/public-api/posts/create) and its `value[].id`.
