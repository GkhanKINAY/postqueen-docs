---
title: "Delete a post"
sidebarTitle: "Delete a post"
description: "Delete a post by id, with its whole thread or comments on that channel."
openapi: "DELETE /posts/{id}"
---

A delete removes the post's **group**: the post on one channel with every entry of its thread or its comments. Deleting the first entry of a thread removes the whole thread, and so does the id of one of its comments. The same content on your other channels is not touched, because each channel entry of a create request gets its own group.

A successful delete answers `200` with the body `{ "error": true }`. The name is a quirk of the code, not a failure: judge the call by its status code.

<Tip>
An id that is unknown or already deleted answers `404 Post not found`. A client that retries deletes can treat that `404` as done.
</Tip>

Nothing already published is taken down from the network. To delete by group id instead, use [Delete a post by group](/public-api/posts/delete-by-group).
