description: "Group channels by client, then narrow the calendar and Create Post to one client."

**Where:** **Channels** in the left rail > pick a channel > **Move / add to group**.

A customer is a group of channels that belong to one client. Once your channels are in more than one group, the calendar and Create Post get a customer picker that narrows them to one client.

## Put a channel under a customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the channel">
    Open **Channels** in the left rail and pick the channel. Under **Channel**, choose **Move** on the **Move / add to group** row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the customer's name">
    In **Select Customer**, start typing. Names you already use are suggested. A name that does not exist yet becomes a new customer when you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Choose **Save**. The app confirms with "Customer Updated".
  </Step>
</Steps>

To take a channel out of its customer, open the same window and choose **Remove from customer**. The button shows only when the channel belongs to a customer.

## Show one customer on the calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer picker">
    On the calendar toolbar, choose the person icon (**Select Customer**). A list headed **Customers** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the customer">
    The calendar and the post list show only the posts on that customer's channels. The choice stays in the page address, so you can bookmark it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Start a post for one customer

In Create Post, the same person icon sits next to the channel avatars. Picking a customer replaces your channel selection with every channel of that customer, and the app shows "Customer socials selected". You can still add or remove channels one by one afterwards.

## Good to know

- The picker appears only when your channels span more than one group. Channels without a customer count as a group of their own, so the picker hides when every channel belongs to the same single customer.
- The list of customers has no "all" entry. Pick another customer to switch.
- A channel belongs to one customer at a time. Names match exactly, so "Acme" and "acme co" are two customers.
- The **Channels** list does not group or label channels by customer.
- A channel that a client connects through **Invite by link** arrives without a customer. Put it under one with **Move / add to group**. See [Connect a channel](/using/connect-a-channel).
- Customers are created and assigned only in the app. The API, the CLI and MCP can list them and filter channels and posts by them: see [List Groups (Customers)](/public-api/integrations/groups), [Managing Posts](/cli/managing-posts) and the [MCP tools](/mcp/tools).
