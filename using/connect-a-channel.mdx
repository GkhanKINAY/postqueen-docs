**Where:** **Channels** in the left rail > **Add Channel**.

A channel is one account on one network that PostQueen posts to. Connect at least one before you create a post. [Channels](/channels/overview) lists which networks you can connect today and which are **In review** or **Soon**.

## Add a channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add Channel">
    Open **Channels** in the left rail and choose **Add Channel**. With no channels yet, the **Add a channel** pane opens by itself, and **Create Post** in the header opens it too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the network">
    Choosing a network opens a short setup step that says what it needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in, or enter the details">
    Most networks show **Continue to** and the network's name, which takes you to that network's own sign-in to approve PostQueen. Networks that connect with a key or password, such as Bluesky, WordPress, DEV and Nostr, show **Enter details**: fill in the form and choose **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that it arrived">
    You land back on **Channels** with the new channel selected and a **Connected** badge. If the network asks you to pick a page or account after you sign in, finish that step first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Let the account owner connect it

When a client owns the account, choose **Invite by link** instead of **Connect myself**, pick the network and choose **Copy Link**. The link works for one hour, and the channel lands in your workspace when the owner finishes. Only networks that sign in on their own page are offered this way.

## Reconnect a channel

When a network's sign-in expires or is revoked, the channel shows **Needs reconnecting** in the list, and its page says "This channel lost its connection and will not publish until you reconnect it." Choose **Reconnect** and sign in again. A network that uses a key or password asks for the details again. The channel keeps its posts and time slots.

## Disable or delete a channel

Both are under **Access** on the channel's page.

- **Disable Channel** keeps the channel but stops publishing to it. A post that comes due on a disabled channel fails with "Channel disabled". **Enable Channel** turns it back on while your enabled channels are under your plan's limit.
- **Delete channel** removes the channel and its posts. The confirm dialog says exactly what goes:

<Warning>
"Deleting this channel also deletes all of its posts in PostQueen, including scheduled posts, drafts and published posts. Scheduled posts will not go out, and reconnecting the channel later does not bring the posts back. Posts that were already published are not taken down from the platform."
</Warning>

Posts you wrote for several channels at once keep their copies on your other channels.

## Channel limits

Each plan allows a number of channels; [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans) has the numbers. Connecting a new channel counts every channel except those that need reconnecting. Disabled channels count too, so disabling one does not make room. Moving to a plan with fewer channels disables the newest channels over the limit; see [Manage your subscription](/cloud/subscription).

## Good to know

- The channel's page also holds **Edit Time Slots** ([Posting times](/using/posting-times)), **Move / add to group** ([Customers](/using/customers)), **Update Credentials**, and the channel ID for the API, MCP and the CLI.
- **New post** on the channel's page opens Create Post with that channel selected. It is off while the channel needs reconnecting.
- During the free trial, X cannot be connected yet. Its setup step shows "X unlocks after your trial", with **End free trial to unlock X**. See [Free trial](/cloud/trial).

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| “You have reached the maximum number of channels for your subscription. Please upgrade your subscription to add more channels.” | Delete a channel you no longer need, or move to a bigger plan on **Billing**. |
| “This social channel was connected previously to another PostQueen account.” | This check runs during the free trial. End the trial with **Fast track - Charge me now**, or connect a different account. |
| “Could not connect to the platform” | Try again. If it keeps failing, [contact support](/support). |
| “Refresh channel needed” on a failed post | Reconnect the channel, then reschedule the post. |
| “Channel disabled” on a failed post | Choose **Enable Channel**, then reschedule the post. |

[A post did not go out](/troubleshooting/post-failed) covers failed posts in more detail.
