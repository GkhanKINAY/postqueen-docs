**Where:** **Channels** in the left rail > pick a channel > **Edit Time Slots**.

Each channel keeps a short list of times of day, its time slots. When PostQueen picks a time for you from the slots, it takes the next one that no post in the workspace already uses.

## Edit a channel's time slots

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the channel">
    Open **Channels** in the left rail and pick the channel. Under **Channel**, choose **Edit** on the **Edit Time Slots** row. The **Time Table Slots** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a time">
    Under **Add Time Slot**, pick the **Hour** and the **Minutes**, then choose **Add**. The time joins the list below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a time">
    Hover a time and choose the trash icon, then confirm "Are you sure you want to delete this slot?"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Choose **Save changes**. A channel needs at least one slot: with none, the button stays off and the window says "Add at least one time slot to save."
  </Step>
</Steps>

The times are in your time zone. A teammate in another time zone sees the same moments in their own time.

## What a new channel starts with

A channel you connect gets three slots: 09:20, 14:10 and 19:00 in your time zone at the moment you connect it. Replace them with the times you want. Reconnecting a channel keeps its slots.

## What picks a time from the slots

| Where | Which slots it reads |
|---|---|
| **New post** on a channel's page | That channel's slots |
| **AI post** in the **Create Post** menu | The slots of every enabled channel |
| [RSS Auto Post](/using/rss-auto-post), for the drafts and posts it creates | The slots of every enabled channel |
| [Find a free slot over the API](/public-api/integrations/find-slot) | The slots of the channel you ask about |

<Note>
**Create Post** in the header and **Duplicate Post** do not use the slots. They open at the first quarter hour between one and four hours from now that no post uses, so a new post is never parked days ahead.
</Note>

## Good to know

- A slot counts as taken when any post in the workspace sits at exactly that minute, on any channel and in any state, drafts included. A post at 09:21 leaves the 09:20 slot free.
- When every slot for today is past or taken, PostQueen looks at the next day.
- A disabled channel's slots are ignored.
- The slot is only where the date starts. You can change the date and time in the composer before you schedule.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| “Could not save the posting times” | Nothing was saved. Try **Save changes** again. |
| “Add at least one time slot to save.” | Add a time before you save. |
