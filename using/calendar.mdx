**Where:** **Calendar** in the left rail opens the week. **Posts** in the rail opens the same posts as a list.

Every post sits here at the time it goes out, including posts an agent, the CLI or the API created. Check what is coming up, move a post, or start one in an empty slot.

## Pick a view

The **Day**, **Week** and **Month** switch sets the grid; the calendar opens on **Week**. **Today** returns to now.

- **Day** shows all 24 hours. An empty hour ahead reads **Add a post at** and the time.
- **Week** shows as many cards as fit in an hour, then **See all N posts**, which opens that day in the list.
- **Month** shows three posts a day, then **+N more**, which opens that day in the list.

An empty slot in the past reads **Date passed**: you cannot start or drop a post there.

## Use the Posts list

The list groups posts under their date, 100 to a page, with **Previous** and **Next**.

| Control | Options |
|---|---|
| Status tabs | **All**, **Scheduled** (upcoming only), **Drafts**, **Posted** |
| Date menu | **All dates**, **Today**, **This week**, **Next 3 days**, **Past only** (not under Scheduled), **Pick a date…** |
| Sort | **Newest** (the default) or **Oldest** |

Beside the grid, the **Posts** panel has **Scheduled**, **Drafts** and **Posted** tabs. **Hide posts** folds it away and **Show posts** brings it back. Drag a scheduled post from the grid onto the panel and it becomes a draft ("Moved to drafts").

Narrow the view with **All channels** (pick the channels to show) and, once your channels are in more than one [customer](/using/customers) group, **Select Customer**.

## Move a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drag the card">
    Drag a post onto another slot that is not in the past. In Month view, drop it on a day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the new time">
    A scheduled post shows "Scheduled for" and its new time. A draft stays a draft; only its date changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer for a post that already went out">
    If the post was published, or its time has passed, PostQueen asks "This post was already published, what do you want to do?". **Just update the post details** changes the date only. **Reschedule the post** puts it back in the queue, so it is published again at the new time. Closing the dialog cancels the move.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage a post

Click a card to open it in the composer. Hover it for these actions:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Edit** | Opens the post in the composer. |
| **Duplicate Post** | Opens a copy in the composer at the next free time. |
| **Go to post** | Opens the published post on the network. Shown once it is live. |
| **Preview Post** | Opens the post's preview page in a new tab, where **Share with a client** copies the link. The page shows published posts only; before that it reads "Post not found". Members of your workspace can sign in there and leave comments. |
| **Delete Post** | Asks "Are you sure you want to delete this post?", then deletes the post and its comments or thread parts on that channel. Copies of the post on your other channels stay. |

In the Posts list, a published post also has **Post Statistics**. Channel-wide numbers are on [Analytics](/using/analytics).

## Start a post from an empty slot

Click an empty slot to open the composer at that time; in the current hour it starts ten minutes from now. A day in Month view starts at a random hour, so set the time before you save. With no channels connected, the calendar offers **Add your first channel** instead.

## Good to know

- Times show in your time zone. Choose **AM:PM** or **24 hours** under **Settings > Global Settings > Date Metrics**.
- A card's colored stripe takes the color of its first [tag](/using/sets-signatures-and-tags), or else its status. Drafts carry a **Draft** label.
- A repeating post appears on every repeat inside the range you are looking at.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Could not move this post, please try again" | The card goes back to its old time. Try again, or open the post and change the date there. |
| "Could not delete the post." | Reload the page and try again. |
| A card with the **Error** status | The post did not go out. See [A post did not go out](/troubleshooting/post-failed). |
