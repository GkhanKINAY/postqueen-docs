**Where:** **Settings** at the bottom of the left rail, or in the account menu under your avatar.

Settings opens over the page you were on and starts on **Global Settings**. Its tabs sit in three groups, and a few appear only for your role or your plan. Type in **Search settings** to filter the list.

## The tabs

| Group | Tab | What it holds | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workspace | **Global Settings** | **Organization name**, **Date Metrics**, **Date format** and **Shortlink Settings** | Everyone |
| Workspace | **Teams** | Members, roles and invites. See [Team](/using/team) | Admins and Super Admins |
| Workspace | **Language** | The language for the interface, emails and AI prompts | Everyone |
| Workspace | **Notifications** | Your email notifications | Everyone |
| More | **Social Sets**, **Signatures** | Saved starting points and text for posts. See [Sets, signatures and tags](/using/sets-signatures-and-tags) | Paid plans |
| More | **Auto Post** | Feeds that turn into posts. See [RSS Auto Post](/using/rss-auto-post) | Paid plans |
| More | **Webhooks** | Calls to your URL when a post goes out. See [Webhooks](/using/webhooks) | Paid plans |
| More | **Integrations** | Services you connect with their own API key to bring media into posts. See [AI images](/using/ai-images-and-video) | Everyone |
| Account | **Account** | Your name, email, password, sign-in accounts, time zone and **Delete Account** | Everyone |
| Account | **API Keys**, **Developers**, **Approved Apps** | The same screens as on [Connections](/using/connections) | Everyone; only admins can reveal the key or create an app |

On **Global Settings**:

- Only the Super Admin sees **Organization name**, the workspace name shown in the switcher and on invoices.
- **Date Metrics** (**AM:PM** or **24 hours**) and **Date format** (**MM/DD/YYYY** or **DD/MM/YYYY**) are saved in this browser only.
- **Shortlink Settings** decides what happens to links in your posts: **Ask every time**, **Always shortlink** or **Never shortlink**. Only an admin can change it.

## Choose your email notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notifications">
    In Settings, choose **Notifications** in the Workspace group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch each email on or off">
    **Success Emails** tell you a post was published, **Failure Emails** that a post failed, and **Streak Reminder Emails** that your posting streak is about to end. All three start on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

These are yours alone: each member picks the emails for their own inbox. Keep **Failure Emails** on unless you check the calendar every day, because a failed post does not publish again by itself. See [A post did not go out](/troubleshooting/post-failed).

## Change your account details

In **Settings > Account**:

- **Profile**: choose **Edit** next to **Name**, then **Save**.
- **Email**: choose **Change**, enter the **New email** and choose **Send confirmation**. The address changes when you confirm it from the new inbox.
- **Password**: choose **Change**, or **Set password** if you only sign in with another account. Changing it signs out your other sessions.
- **Connected accounts**: **Connect** or **Unlink** a sign-in method. You cannot unlink the last one unless a password is set.

## Delete your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Delete Account">
    In **Settings > Account**, scroll to **Delete Account** and choose it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it is you">
    Type your email in **Type your email to confirm** and enter your **Current password**. If your account has no password, sign out and sign in again with your connected account first. That sign-in counts for 20 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    Choose **Delete Account**, then **Yes, delete my account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
This cannot be undone. Every workspace you own is deleted with its channels and posts, and its paid plan is canceled first. Workspaces you only belong to carry on without you. For what happens to your data afterward, see [Security and privacy](/security-and-privacy).
</Warning>

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| “Please remove your team members before deleting your account” | In each workspace you own, remove the other members in **Teams**, or make an Admin the Super Admin with **Transfer**. |
| “Current password is incorrect” | Enter the password you sign in with. |
| “Re-authenticate with a connected account to delete your account” | Sign out, sign in again with your connected account, then delete within 20 minutes. |
| “Could not cancel your subscription, please try again or contact support” | Try again. If it repeats, email [support@postqueen.ai](mailto:support@postqueen.ai). |
| “Name must be at least 3 characters” | Use a longer name. |
| “Password must be at least 8 characters” | Use a longer password. |
