Settings is one page with a row of tabs, and which tabs you see depends on your plan and your
role. That is the part that confuses people: a tab named in a guide can be genuinely absent from
your own screen, and nothing explains why.

This page is the map. Each tab can be linked to directly with `?tab=`, which is how the rest of
these docs send you to a specific one.

## The tabs

| Tab | What is in it | You see it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Global Settings** | Workspace name (Super Admin only), date format, and whether she offers to shorten links | Always |
| **Language** | The interface language | Always |
| **Teams** | Invite people, change User/Admin roles, transfer Super Admin, or leave | Your plan includes team members, **and** you are an organization admin |
| **Account** | Your name, email, password, connected Google/GitHub/Apple (and other enabled OAuth) accounts, and **Delete Account** | Always (first item in the Account group) |
| **Webhooks** | URLs she calls when a post publishes or fails | Your plan includes webhooks |
| **Auto Post** | RSS feeds that turn new articles into scheduled posts on their own | Your plan includes auto posting |
| **Social Sets** | Saved groups of channels, so you stop reselecting the same five | Any plan above Free |
| **Signatures** | Blocks of text appended to posts, per channel | Any plan above Free |
| **API Keys** | The workspace public API key. Reveal, copy and rotate it here | The tab is visible to every member. Revealing or rotating the key is an admin action |
| **Developers** | OAuth apps you build for other PostQueen users | Your plan includes the public API. Creating or rotating the client secret is an admin action |
| **Approved Apps** | Third-party apps you have authorized against your account | Always |

<Note>
**Self-hosting with billing off?** Every plan gate above passes, because your account reports
itself as AGENCY, the tier labelled Ultimate, when no Stripe keys are set. So you see all of them.
[Plans and limits](/using/plans-and-limits) has the rule.
</Note>

## Why a tab is missing

Two different reasons, and they are worth telling apart before you go looking for a bug.

**Your plan does not include it.** Webhooks, Auto Post, Social Sets, Signatures and Developers (OAuth)
are each gated on a plan feature. The tab is simply not rendered.

**Your role is not admin.** Teams requires you to be an **ADMIN** or **SUPERADMIN** in the
organization. API Keys and Developers still appear for members, but revealing the workspace key
or creating an OAuth app is admin-only: a member sees the screen and a note to ask an admin,
rather than an error.

If a teammate can reveal the API key and you cannot, role is the likely difference rather than billing.

## Account

**Account** is the first row in the Account group (`?tab=account`). It is your user, not the
workspace:

- **Name.** Saved with `POST /user/personal`. At least three characters.
- **Email.** Shown read-only. Change sends a confirmation to the new address (20 minutes) and a
  notice to the old one. The login email does not move until you confirm. Another account already
  using that inbox is refused.
- **Password.** At least eight characters. If you already have one, the current password is
  required; other sessions are signed out. OAuth-only accounts set a password after a confirmation
  email or a fresh sign-in with a connected account.
- **Connected accounts.** Google, GitHub, Apple, and any other OAuth provider enabled on the
  install. You cannot unlink the last sign-in method unless a password is set.

Workspace name lives on **Global Settings**, and only a Super Admin can change it.

## Teams

The member list is **Member | Role | Actions**. Super Admin is a badge. User and Admin can be
changed when your role is strictly above theirs — an Admin cannot change another Admin. Super Admin
cannot be granted from this list; ownership moves with **Transfer** (the current Super Admin
becomes Admin). You can leave the workspace unless you are the last Super Admin. Inviting an email
that is already in the workspace is refused.

## Deleting your account

**Delete Account** sits at the bottom of **Account** (not Global Settings). On the Free tier the
billing page still points at the same screen. Type your email to enable the button, then confirm
with your password or a fresh OAuth sign-in. After that there is no undo.

What goes, and what stays behind:

- **Organizations you own** are deleted, together with their posts and channels.
- **Organizations you only belong to** carry on. You are removed from their team.
- **Your own account** is emptied rather than kept: your email address, password, name, linked
  identities and profile details are scrubbed, and you are signed out.

Two things refuse the request:

- **Other people in an organization you own.** Remove them first. Until you do, the answer is
  *Please remove your team members before deleting your account*.
- **Impersonation.** An admin viewing the app as you cannot delete your account for you.

A paid subscription on an organization you own is cancelled first. If that cancellation fails,
nothing is deleted, and you can try again.

## Linking straight to a tab

Append `?tab=` and the tab's key:

```
https://app.postqueen.ai/settings?tab=account
https://app.postqueen.ai/settings?tab=api
https://app.postqueen.ai/settings?tab=teams
https://app.postqueen.ai/settings?tab=autopost
```

The keys are `global_settings`, `language`, `teams`, `account`, `webhooks`, `autopost`, `sets`,
`signatures`, `api`, `developers` and `approved_apps`. **Account** is `account`. **API Keys** is
`api`. **Developers** (OAuth apps) is `developers`. An unrecognised or unavailable key falls back
to Global Settings rather than erroring.
