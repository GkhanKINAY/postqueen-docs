**Where:** **Settings** > **Auto Post**, in the More group. The rail's **More** group links there too.

Auto Post checks a feed every hour. When the feed has a new item, PostQueen writes a post with the item's link and puts it on your calendar, as a draft for you to review or as a scheduled post. Every paid plan includes it.

## Add a feed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Add an autopost">
    The form has four steps: **Feed**, **Timing**, **Content** and **Channels**. You need a connected channel first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Feed: name it and test it">
    Enter a **Title** for your own use and the feed's **URL**, then choose **Send Test**. "RSS valid!" means PostQueen can read the feed. **Save** stays locked until the test passes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Timing and Content">
    Choose **Next** and answer each question, as in the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Channels">
    Choose **All channels**, or **Specific channels** and tick the ones you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Save">
    "Autopost added successfully" confirms it. The first check runs right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Step | Question | What the answers do |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | **Should we sync the current last post?** | **Yes** posts the item at the top of the feed on the first check. **No** marks it as seen, so only items published after it are posted. |
| Timing | **When should we post it?** | **Post on the next available slot** or **Post Immediately**. Today both put the post at the next free posting time. |
| Timing | **What should we do with new items?** | **Create a draft for review** (the default) or **Publish automatically**. See below. |
| Content | **Autogenerate content** | **Yes**: PostQueen writes a short post of about 100 characters from the item. **No**: every item uses your text from **Post content**. |
| Content | **Generate Picture?** | **Yes**: PostQueen generates an image for each item. |

## What happens to each new item

- Each hour PostQueen takes the newest item in the feed. If it took that item last time, nothing happens. When several items appear within one hour, only the newest is posted.
- The post is the text, a blank line, then the item's link.
- It goes on your calendar at the next free [posting time](/using/posting-times).
- With **Create a draft for review**, every chosen channel gets a draft. Nothing goes out until you open the draft and schedule it.
- With **Publish automatically**, the post is scheduled on each channel where it passes that network's checks. A channel that needs settings of its own, such as a title, gets a draft instead, and a notification names it. Channels that are disabled or need reconnecting are skipped.

<Tip>
For **Publish automatically**, pick channels that need nothing but text and a link, such as Bluesky or Nostr. A network that asks for a title or other settings on every post always falls back to a draft.
</Tip>

## Good to know

- Each saved feed has a toggle that pauses the hourly check and keeps the setup.
- **Test connection** on a saved feed reads it again and changes nothing.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Could not use this RSS feed" | The address is not a feed PostQueen can read, or the newest item has no link. Open the address in a browser and check it. |
| "URL must be a public HTTPS URL and cannot point to internal network addresses" | Use the feed's public `https://` address. |
| "Run Send Test on the Feed step before saving." | Go back to **Feed** and choose **Send Test**. |
| "Pick at least one channel." | Tick a channel, or choose **All channels**. |
| "Autopost saved drafts instead of publishing" (notification) | The channels it names could not publish without their own settings. Open the drafts on the calendar and finish them. |
| "Autopost has no channel to post to" (notification) | None of the feed's channels can be used. Reconnect them, or edit the feed and pick other channels. |
| "Your subscription does not include Auto Post. Please upgrade your subscription to post from a feed automatically." | Auto Post needs a paid plan. See [Plans](/cloud/plans). |
