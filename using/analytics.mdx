**Where:** **Analytics** in the left rail.

Analytics shows what the networks report about your channels and your published posts. Nine networks send numbers back. Channels on every other network publish normally but do not appear on this page.

## The nine networks that report numbers

| Network | Status today | Periods | Per-post numbers | Followers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [X](/channels/x-twitter) | In review | 7, 30, 90 days | Yes | No |
| [Facebook Page](/channels/facebook) | In review | 7, 30, 90 days | Yes | Yes |
| [Instagram](/channels/instagram) | In review | 7, 30 days | Yes | Yes |
| [LinkedIn Page](/channels/linkedin) | Soon | 7, 30, 90 days | Yes | Yes |
| [YouTube](/channels/youtube) | In review | 7, 30, 90 days | Yes | Subscribers |
| [Threads](/channels/threads) | In review | 7, 30 days | Yes | No |
| [Pinterest](/channels/pinterest) | Soon | 7, 30, 90 days | Impressions only | No |
| [TikTok](/channels/tiktok) | In review | 7, 30 days | Yes | Yes |
| [Google Business Profile](/channels/google-my-business) | Soon | 7, 30, 90 days | No | No |

<Note>
None of the networks you can connect without limits today reports analytics. A network **In review** works only within the limit its page states, and a **Soon** network cannot be connected yet. [Channels](/channels/overview) has the status of every network.
</Note>

## Read your numbers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick what to look at">
    With more than one reporting channel, the page opens on **All channels**. Pick a single channel in the list to see only its numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the period">
    Choose **7d**, **30d** or **90d** at the top. A channel offers only the periods its network supports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open one post">
    In **Performance per post**, choose the statistics icon on a row. From **Posts** in the left rail, choose the **Post Statistics** icon on a published post. The **Statistics** window shows that post's numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each view shows

**All channels** covers your published posts across the reporting channels:

- Your top posts, up to five, ranked by **Reactions** or **Comments**.
- **By channel**: post impressions in this period, per channel.
- **Posting days**: how many posts went out on each weekday.
- **Engagement mix**: known reactions against comments.
- **Performance per post**: a table you can sort by **Reactions**, **Comments**, **Eng. rate** and **Impressions**, with **Previous** and **Next** to page through it.

The period picks which posts count, by the day they were published. The numbers are each post's totals so far, not only the activity inside the period.

**One channel** shows a card for each metric the network returns, with a chart and a total for the period. When the network has per-post numbers, the post sections above follow for that channel alone.

## Good to know

- A channel marked **Needs reconnecting** cannot load numbers. Choosing it takes you to **Channels** to reconnect it.
- The metric names on the cards come from each network, so they differ from one network to the next.
- With no reporting channel yet, the page says "No analytics yet" and offers **Connect a channel**.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| A note that the channel needs to be refreshed, with **Refresh Channel** | Choose **Refresh Channel** and sign in to the network again. |
| “No data in this period” | The network reported nothing for this period. Pick a longer one. |
| “Could not load analytics” | Choose **Try again**. |
| “Could not load your channels” | Check your connection, then choose **Try again**. |
