Each plan on PostQueen Cloud has a fixed cap on connected channels (a
"channel" is one connected social account, e.g. one Twitter user, one
Discord server). When you hit the cap, adding a new channel returns:

> You have reached the maximum number of channels

## How to free a slot

Disabling a channel does not free a slot for a new connection. Every connected channel counts
toward the cap, disabled ones included. Only a channel that is waiting for a reconnect is left
out of the count. Disabling a channel only makes room to turn another disabled channel back on.

To connect a new channel at the cap, either raise the cap (below) or delete a channel you no
longer need.

<Warning>
Deleting a channel also deletes all of that channel's posts: scheduled posts, drafts and published
history, with their comments. There is no undo. [Connect a channel](/using/connect-a-channel)
covers the difference between Disable and Delete.
</Warning>

## How to raise the cap

Upgrade your plan on the [Billing page](https://app.postqueen.ai/billing).
Each tier raises the channel limit. Numbers: [Cloud plans](/cloud/plans) and [Limits](/cloud/limits).

## Self-host

Self-hosted PostQueen has no channel cap **while billing is off**, which is the normal state:
billing only turns on when both `STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` and `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` are set. With
it off, your account reports itself as AGENCY, the tier labelled Ultimate, and no quota is
evaluated, so you can connect as many channels as the providers allow per OAuth app.

Set both Stripe keys and the tiers above apply to your install too.
[Plans and limits](/using/plans-and-limits) has the full rule.
