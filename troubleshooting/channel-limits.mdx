---
title: Channel Limits (Cloud)
description: '"You have reached the maximum number of channels"'
---

Each plan on PostQueen Cloud has a fixed cap on connected channels (a
"channel" is one connected social account, e.g. one Twitter user, one
Discord server). When you hit the cap, adding a new channel returns:

> You have reached the maximum number of channels

## How to free a slot

1. Open **Channels** in the PostQueen app.
2. Find a channel you no longer need.
3. Click **Disable** (or **Disconnect** / **Remove**, depending on UI version).
   This frees the slot immediately. You do not need to delete past posts.

If you want to keep the channel's history accessible but stop posting
to it, **Disable** is enough; the data stays in your account.

## How to raise the cap

Upgrade your plan on the [Billing page](https://app.postqueen.ai/billing).
Each tier raises the channel limit; the exact numbers are visible on the
plan comparison.

## Self-host

Self-hosted PostQueen has no channel cap **while billing is off**, which is the normal state:
billing only turns on when both `STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` and `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` are set. With
it off, your account reports itself as ULTIMATE and no quota is evaluated, so you can connect as
many channels as the providers allow per OAuth app.

Set both Stripe keys and the tiers above apply to your install too.
[Plans and limits](/using/plans-and-limits) has the full rule.
