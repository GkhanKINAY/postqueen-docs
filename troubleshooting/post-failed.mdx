**Where:** the calendar, the **All** tab of the Posts list, and your notifications.

A post that could not be published is marked with a red **!** on the calendar, and PostQueen tells you why. Once PostQueen stops trying, nothing publishes the post again by itself: you fix the cause, then send it again.

| What you see | Go to |
|---|---|
| A red **!** on a post | [Read why it failed](#read-why-it-failed) |
| “We couldn't confirm your post on ...” | [Sent, but not confirmed](#sent-but-not-confirmed) |
| “Could not refresh your ... channel” | [The channel needs reconnecting](#the-channel-needs-reconnecting) |
| A post that still says **Publishing** | [Not failed yet](#not-failed-yet) |
| You fixed the cause | [Send the post again](#send-the-post-again) |

## Read why it failed

In **Day** and **Week** view, a failed post has a red ring and a red **!**. In **Month** view it has a red outline only, so switch to **Week** to read the reason. Hover the **!** to see it: the message PostQueen kept for the post, up to 300 characters. When there is no readable message, it says "An error occurred while publishing this post".

In the Posts list, failed posts are only under **All**, marked **Error**. **Scheduled** shows posts still ahead of you, so a failed post never appears there.

Most reasons are the network's own words. These come from PostQueen:

| Message | What it means and what to do |
|---|---|
| “Refresh channel needed” | The channel had lost its connection before the post's time. [Reconnect the channel](#the-channel-needs-reconnecting), then send the post again. |
| “Channel disabled” | The channel was turned off. Choose **Enable Channel** on the channel's page, then send the post again. |
| “Channel setup not finished” | Connecting the channel stopped before a step such as picking a page. Open the channel on **Channels**, finish connecting it, then send the post again. |
| “This channel cannot post comments” | The post had follow-up parts, and this network has no way to add them. Only the follow-ups are marked; the first part is not affected. |
| “Subscription required” | The workspace had no active plan when the post was due. Renew the plan on **Billing**, then send the post again. |
| “No Post” | The post was gone when its time came, usually because it was deleted. Create it again if you still want it. |
| “A previous publish attempt was interrupted”, “Could not confirm the post status”, “Could not publish after several attempts” | The post may have reached the network: [Sent, but not confirmed](#sent-but-not-confirmed). |

Anything else came from the network. Its page lists the rules PostQueen checks before a post is scheduled: start from [All channels](/channels/overview), fix the post, then send it again. An unchanged post that the network refused is refused again.

## How PostQueen retries

PostQueen makes up to 5 attempts at one post. It tries again when the channel's sign-in had expired and was renewed, or when an attempt could not start. It stops at once when:

- the network refused the content, for example its length or its media;
- the channel's sign-in could not be renewed, so the channel [needs reconnecting](#the-channel-needs-reconnecting);
- the post was sent but no answer came back, so it may be live: [Sent, but not confirmed](#sent-but-not-confirmed).

<Note>
A post is marked failed after its first failed attempt, not its last. If a later attempt works, the same post turns into a published one. Give a fresh failure a few minutes before you send it again.
</Note>

## Sent, but not confirmed

Sometimes the post reached the network, but PostQueen could not confirm that it went live: the network did not answer in time, or never gave a final answer. The post is marked failed, and you get this notice:

> Your post was sent to *network*, but we couldn't confirm it was published. Please check your *channel* account before posting again to avoid duplicates.

<Warning>
**Check the network before you send it again.** The post may already be live, and sending it again publishes it twice.
</Warning>

## Not failed yet

- **Publishing.** A scheduled post whose time has passed shows **Publishing** until the network confirms it. Some networks accept a post and publish it later. PostQueen then checks every 20 seconds for about 30 minutes, and says so in your notifications: "*Network* accepted your post and is still processing it. It will be confirmed here once it is live."
- **Missed while PostQueen was busy.** Once an hour, PostQueen looks for scheduled posts from the last two days that never ran, on channels that are connected and turned on, and publishes them. It never picks up a failed post.
- **Draft.** A draft keeps its date on the calendar but never publishes until you schedule it.

## The channel needs reconnecting

When a network stops accepting the channel's sign-in and PostQueen cannot renew it, you get "Could not refresh your *network* channel (*account*). Reconnect it to keep publishing." The channel shows **Needs reconnecting** on **Channels**, and every post due on it fails with "Refresh channel needed" until you reconnect it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the channel">
    Follow the link in the notice, or open **Channels** and choose the channel marked **Needs reconnecting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconnect it">
    Choose **Reconnect** and sign in to the network again. A network that uses a key or a password asks for it again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send each failed post again">
    The posts that failed while the channel was disconnected do not resume by themselves. [Send each one again](#send-the-post-again).
  </Step>
</Steps>

[Connect a channel](/using/connect-a-channel#reconnect-a-channel) has more on reconnecting.

## Send the post again

<Warning>
A post keeps its date. If that date has passed, it is published right away. Move it to a later time if you want another moment. If the post was [sent but not confirmed](#sent-but-not-confirmed), check the network first.
</Warning>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Drag it on the calendar">
    Drag the failed post onto a slot in the future. PostQueen does not ask you to confirm: it shows "Scheduled for" with the new time and publishes the post then.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Edit and update it">
    Click the post to open it, fix what the network refused, and choose **Update**. It is scheduled again at its date. To publish it now instead, open the arrow next to **Update** and choose **Post now**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="The API, the CLI or an agent">
    Changing the status of a failed post is refused with "Cannot change status of a published or errored post". Create the post again with `POST /posts`, `postqueen posts:create` or your agent, or send it again from the calendar.

    To check for failed posts from a script, list your posts with `GET /posts` or `postqueen posts:list`: a failed one has the state `ERROR`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Notifications about failed posts

A failed post writes a notification in the app, and most of these are also emailed.

| Notification | Emailed |
|---|---|
| “An error occurred while posting on *network*:” and the network's message | When **Failure Emails** is on |
| “We couldn't publish your post to *channel*:” and the reason | When **Failure Emails** is on |
| “Your post was sent to *network*, but we couldn't confirm it was published.” | When **Failure Emails** is on |
| “Could not refresh your *network* channel” | Always |
| “We couldn't post to *network* for *channel* because it's disabled.” and the other channel notices | Always |
| “Your post to *channel* was not published because your subscription is no longer active.” | Always |
| “... accepted your post and is still processing it.” | Never, in the app only |

Turn **Failure Emails** on or off in [Settings](/using/settings) under **Notifications**. A later failed attempt at the same post does not notify you again.

## Still stuck?

Email [support@postqueen.ai](mailto:support@postqueen.ai) with:

- the channel, and the time the post was due, with your time zone;
- the reason in the tooltip, word for word, and the notification you got;
- the post's ID, if you use the API or the CLI.

The tooltip shows up to 300 characters. Support can read the full message PostQueen kept for the post.
