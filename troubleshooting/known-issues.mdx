**Last reviewed on 23 September 2026.** Each entry was checked against the app on that day. An entry is removed once its fix is live.

Only issues you can meet on PostQueen today are listed. A network marked Soon cannot be connected, so its bugs are not here.

## Posts and the calendar

| What happens | Way around it |
|---|---|
| **Preview Post** on a post that is not published yet opens a page that says "Post not found". The preview page shows published posts only. | Check an unpublished post in the composer. Once it is live, **Preview Post** works, and **Go to post** opens it on the network. |
| The **Add a thread finisher** switch on X, Threads and Bluesky is never published. | Write the closing text as the last part of the thread. |
| A post started with **Create Post** at the top does not get the signature marked **Auto add signature?**. Only a post started from an empty calendar slot gets it. | Start the post from an empty slot, or add the signature with the signature button in the editor. [Sets, signatures and tags](/using/sets-signatures-and-tags#add-a-signature) |
| An upload cannot be canceled once it starts: the uploader shows its progress only. | Let it finish, then delete the file from the [media library](/using/media-library). |

## Channels

| What happens | Way around it |
|---|---|
| **Enable Channel** says "Channel Enabled" when your enabled channels are already at your plan's limit, but the channel stays off. | Disable another channel first, or move to a bigger plan, then enable it again. Reload **Channels** to check that it is on. [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans) |
| A channel connected while your time zone is UTC+0 gets the posting times 02:00, 06:40 and 11:40 instead of 09:20, 14:10 and 19:00. | Set the times you want with **Edit Time Slots** on the channel's page. [Posting times](/using/posting-times) |
| On Lemmy, the settings say "Please add at least one Subreddit" until you pick a community. It means a Lemmy community. | Choose **Add Community** and pick one. [Lemmy](/channels/lemmy) |
| The Listmonk connect form says the token is under Settings → API users. In current Listmonk, API users are under **Admin > Users**. | Follow the steps on [Listmonk](/channels/listmonk). |

## Moltbook

<Warning>
Moltbook support is experimental. Moltbook keeps a new post out of its feeds until the agent answers a verification challenge, and PostQueen does not answer it. Your posts may not show up in feeds. [Moltbook](/channels/moltbook) has the details.
</Warning>

| What happens | Way around it |
|---|---|
| A post with no **Submolt** fails in the composer with "Could not save the post, please try again". | Fill in **Submolt**, such as `general`. |
| Over the API, [Integration settings](/public-api/integrations/settings) answers "No additional settings required" for a Moltbook channel, but `POST /posts` needs `submolt`. | Always send `"submolt"` in the settings: [Moltbook posting settings](/public-api/providers/moltbook). |

## Automation

| What happens | Way around it |
|---|---|
| In [RSS Auto Post](/using/rss-auto-post), **Post Immediately** puts new items at the next free posting time, like **Post on the next available slot**. | Add more posting times to the channel so the next free one comes sooner. |
| On Bluesky, both [Auto-Plugs](/using/auto-plugs) actions stop after the first check, about 6 hours after the post publishes, although the card says the post is checked three times. | Set a number of likes a good post reaches within a few hours. |

## Plans

| What happens | Way around it |
|---|---|
| The plan card on **Billing** lists an Advanced Picture Editor. It is not available in the app yet. | Nothing to do. [AI images](/using/ai-images-and-video) covers the image tools you can use today. |

## API

| What happens | Way around it |
|---|---|
| A few endpoints answer `500` instead of `404` for a wrong ID, such as `PUT /posts/{id}/release-id` and `DELETE /integrations/{id}`. | Retrying does not help. Check the ID. [When a 500 is a wrong id](/public-api/limits-and-errors#when-a-500-is-a-wrong-id) lists them. |
| A post that names a media `id` that is not in your media library answers `500` with "Internal server error", not a message. | Send the `id` and `path` your own upload returned. |
| The API accepts X settings that need X Premium (`subscribers` and `verified` replies, and Articles) for an account without Premium. X may then refuse the post when it is published. | Send them only for an X account with Premium. [X posting settings](/public-api/providers/x) |
