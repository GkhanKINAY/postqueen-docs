Only issues you can run into on PostQueen today are listed.

## Posts and the calendar

| What happens | Way around it |
|---|---|
| An upload cannot be canceled once it starts: the uploader shows its progress only. | Let it finish, then delete the file from the [media library](/using/media-library). |

## Moltbook

<Warning>
Moltbook support is experimental, and your posts may not show up in Moltbook's feeds: [Moltbook](/channels/moltbook) explains why.
</Warning>

| What happens | Way around it |
|---|---|
| A post with no **Submolt** fails in the composer with "Could not save the post, please try again". | Fill in **Submolt**, such as `general`. |
| Over the API, [Integration settings](/public-api/integrations/settings) answers "No additional settings required" for a Moltbook channel, but `POST /posts` needs `submolt`. | Always send `"submolt"` in the settings: [Moltbook posting settings](/public-api/providers/moltbook). |

## Plans

| What happens | Way around it |
|---|---|
| The plan card on **Billing** lists an Advanced Picture Editor. It is not available in the app yet. | Nothing to do. [AI images](/using/ai-images-and-video) covers the image tools you can use today. |

## API

| What happens | Way around it |
|---|---|
| The API accepts X settings that need X Premium (`subscribers` and `verified` replies, and Articles) for an account without Premium. X may then refuse the post when it is published. | Send them only for an X account with Premium. [X posting settings](/public-api/providers/x) |
