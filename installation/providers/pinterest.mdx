On a self-hosted PostQueen the Pinterest app is yours. You request an app from Pinterest, add PostQueen's redirect URI to it, and give PostQueen the app id and secret. Pinterest reviews the app twice: once to create it, and again before Pins are visible to anyone but you.

## Before you start

| | |
|---|---|
| Developer portal | [Pinterest Developers](https://developers.pinterest.com/apps/), with a Pinterest business account |
| Variables | `PINTEREST_CLIENT_ID`, `PINTEREST_CLIENT_SECRET` |
| Redirect URI | `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/pinterest` |
| Scopes | `boards:read`, `boards:write`, `pins:read`, `pins:write`, `user_accounts:read` |
| Review | Pinterest approves the app request for Trial access, then Standard access before Pins are public. See App review |

PostQueen asks for the five scopes when a channel connects, and refuses the connect if Pinterest grants fewer.

## Create the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use a business account">
    Sign in to Pinterest with a business account, or create one: Pinterest says this is the account that administers the app. Verify its email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request the app">
    Open [My apps](https://developers.pinterest.com/apps/) and accept the Developer Terms. Choose **Connect app**, fill in the request form and submit it. Pinterest reviews requests each business day and emails you the decision. An approved app starts on **Trial** access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the redirect URI">
    In **My apps**, choose **Manage** on the app, open the **Configure** tab and find **Redirect URIs**. Enter the address and choose **Add**:

    <CodeGroup>

    ```text Your domain
    https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/pinterest
    ```

    ```text Local development
    http://localhost:4200/integrations/social/pinterest
    ```

    ```text Docker on your machine
    http://localhost:4007/integrations/social/pinterest
    ```

    </CodeGroup>

    Pinterest accepts `https` addresses, and plain `http` only for `localhost`. The address has to match exactly.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/providers/pinterest/pinterest-001.png" alt="The Redirect URIs section of a Pinterest app, with the Redirect link field and the Add button" />
    </Frame>

    <OAuthRedirect />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the app id and secret">
    The app page shows the **App id** and the **App secret key**. Copy both, and keep the secret private.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/providers/pinterest/pinterest-002.png" alt="The App id and App secret key fields of a Pinterest app, with the secret hidden" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the variables

Add the two keys to your environment:

```env
PINTEREST_CLIENT_ID="your app id"
PINTEREST_CLIENT_SECRET="your app secret key"
```

<RestartAndAddChannel />

Pick **Pinterest**, sign in, and approve the board and Pin permissions. The Pinterest account needs at least one board before a Pin can be scheduled.

## App review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trial access">
    A new app can create boards and Pins, but Pinterest treats them as sandbox content that only their creator can see. Writes are capped at 300 a day for the whole app, and all requests at 1,000 a day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Standard access">
    For Pins other people can see, choose **Upgrade** on the app card in **My apps**. Confirm the use case and your privacy policy link, and upload a video of the app at work that shows the sign-in: a screen recording of a channel connecting in PostQueen and a Pin being scheduled is enough, even if you are the only user. Pinterest often refuses an upgrade when the privacy policy cannot be reached or the video does not show the sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On Standard access, Pinterest allows 100 writes a minute for each user. See [Pinterest access tiers](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/key-concepts/access-tiers/) and [rate limits](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/reference/rate-limits/).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Not enough scopes, when choosing a provider, please add all the scopes">
    Pinterest returned a token without all five scopes. Connect again and approve every permission on Pinterest's screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pinterest says the redirect URI does not match">
    The address PostQueen sent is not in **Redirect URIs**. It is exactly your `FRONTEND_URL` followed by `/integrations/social/pinterest`. Check the scheme, the port and that there is no trailing slash.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Apps with Trial access may not create Pins in production">
    Pinterest refuses Pins from an app that is still on Trial access. Request Standard access as described in App review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The board list is empty">
    The Pinterest account has no boards yet. Create one on Pinterest, then open the post again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
