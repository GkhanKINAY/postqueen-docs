On a self-hosted PostQueen you register your own LinkedIn app. One app serves both tiles in **Add Channel**: **LinkedIn** for a personal profile and **LinkedIn Page** for a company page. PostQueen reads the app's keys from two variables.

## Before you start

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Developer portal | [linkedin.com/developers/apps](https://www.linkedin.com/developers/apps) |
| Variables | `LINKEDIN_CLIENT_ID`, `LINKEDIN_CLIENT_SECRET` |
| Redirect URIs | `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/linkedin`, and `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/linkedin-page` |
| Products | Share on LinkedIn, Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect, Advertising API |
| You also need | A LinkedIn Page for your organization, its super admin to verify the app, a privacy policy URL and a logo |

Both tiles request the same seven scopes, and PostQueen refuses the connect if LinkedIn grants fewer:

| Scope | Comes from |
|---|---|
| `openid`, `profile` | Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect |
| `w_member_social` | Share on LinkedIn (the Advertising API grants it too) |
| `r_basicprofile`, `rw_organization_admin`, `w_organization_social`, `r_organization_social` | Advertising API |

The personal **LinkedIn** tile needs the organization scopes as well, so the Advertising API is required even if you never connect a company page.

## Create the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the app">
    In the developer portal, open **My apps** and choose **Create app**. Fill in **App name**, **LinkedIn Page** (your organization's page name or URL), **Privacy policy URL** and **App logo**, accept the legal agreement and choose **Create app**.

    An app stays tied to the LinkedIn Page you pick here. LinkedIn cannot move it to another page later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the app with your LinkedIn Page">
    Open the app's **Settings** tab and choose **Verify**. Choose **Generate URL**, then **Copy URL**, and send the link to a super admin of the page. They approve the request from that link within 30 days. Choose **I'm done** to close the dialog.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/providers/linkedin/linkedin-004.png" alt="The LinkedIn Page box on the app's Settings tab, with the Verify button and the note that the app is not verified yet" />
    </Frame>

    Once the super admin approves, the app shows as verified in its settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the three products">
    Open the **Products** tab:

    - Add **Share on LinkedIn** and **Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect**. Both are added as soon as you accept their terms.
    - Choose **Request access** on **Advertising API** and complete the access form. LinkedIn reviews the request and tells you the decision by email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the scopes">
    Open the **Auth** tab. Once all three products are on the app, the **OAuth 2.0 scopes** list shows all seven scopes from the table above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add both redirect URLs">
    On the **Auth** tab, add both addresses under **Authorized redirect URLs for your app**:

    <OAuthRedirect />

    | Where PostQueen runs | Redirect URLs |
    |---|---|
    | Production | `https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/linkedin` and `https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/linkedin-page` |
    | Local development | `http://localhost:4200/integrations/social/linkedin` and `http://localhost:4200/integrations/social/linkedin-page` |
    | Docker on your machine | `http://localhost:4007/integrations/social/linkedin` and `http://localhost:4007/integrations/social/linkedin-page` |

    LinkedIn ignores query parameters when it compares the address, so a reconnect that adds one still matches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the client ID and secret">
    The **Auth** tab also shows the app's **Client ID** and its client secret. Copy both, and keep the secret private.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the variables

Add the two values to PostQueen's environment:

```env
LINKEDIN_CLIENT_ID="your client id"
LINKEDIN_CLIENT_SECRET="your client secret"
```

<RestartAndAddChannel />

For **LinkedIn Page**, you pick the company page after LinkedIn's consent screen. Only pages where your LinkedIn account is an administrator or content administrator are listed.

## App review

- **Advertising API.** Until LinkedIn approves the request, the app does not have the four organization scopes. Every connect, on both tiles, fails until then.
- **Staying connected.** LinkedIn issues refresh tokens only to apps approved for its marketing products. An access token lasts 60 days and a refresh token 365 days. PostQueen renews the access token by itself; once the refresh token expires, the account has to be reconnected.
- **Community Management API.** It grants the same organization scopes, but LinkedIn lets you request it only on a new app that has no other products, and it starts on a Development tier limited to 500 calls per app a day. The Advertising API is the simpler route for PostQueen.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Not enough scopes, when choosing a provider, please add all the scopes">
    LinkedIn returned a token without all seven scopes. Open the app's **Auth** tab and check the **OAuth 2.0 scopes** list. A missing organization scope means the Advertising API is not approved yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="LinkedIn shows an invalid scope error before its consent screen">
    PostQueen asked for a scope your app does not have yet. Add the missing product, or wait for the Advertising API approval, then connect again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="LinkedIn says the redirect_uri does not match">
    The address PostQueen sent is not in **Authorized redirect URLs for your app**. It is exactly your `FRONTEND_URL` followed by `/integrations/social/linkedin` or `/integrations/social/linkedin-page`. Check the scheme, the port and that there is no trailing slash.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The company page is not in the list">
    LinkedIn Page lists only pages where your LinkedIn account is an administrator or content administrator. Ask a super admin of the page to give you one of those roles, then connect again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
