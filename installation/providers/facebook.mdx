On a self-hosted PostQueen the Meta app is yours. One app, logged in through **Facebook Login for Business**, serves two tiles in **Add Channel**: **Facebook Page** and **Instagram (Facebook Business)**. This page creates that app. **Instagram (Standalone)** and **Threads** each need a separate Meta app: see [Instagram setup](/installation/providers/instagram) and [Threads setup](/installation/providers/threads).

## Before you start

| | |
|---|---|
| Developer console | [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/apps), with a Meta developer account |
| Variables | `FACEBOOK_APP_ID`, `FACEBOOK_APP_SECRET` |
| Redirect URIs | Two paths on `FRONTEND_URL`: `/integrations/social/facebook`, and `/integrations/social/instagram` for Instagram (Facebook Business) |
| Permissions | Seven for Facebook Page, and four more for Instagram (Facebook Business). The list is in step 6 |
| Callback URLs | Two paths on `NEXT_PUBLIC_BACKEND_URL`, for data deletion and deauthorize requests. See step 7 |
| Review | None for Pages you own or manage. App Review and Business Verification for anyone else's Pages |

PostQueen checks every permission it asks for when a Page connects. A missing one stops the connect.

## Create the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new app">
    Open [Create an app](https://developers.facebook.com/apps/creation/) in Meta for Developers. Enter the app name and a contact email, then choose **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Other, not a use case">
    On the use case step, do not pick any of the listed use cases. Scroll to **Looking for something else?**, select **Other** and choose **Next**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/providers/facebook/Other-app-use-cases.png" alt="Meta's Add use cases step, with Other selected under Looking for something else?" />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
    An app created through a use case such as Facebook Login cannot request the permissions PostQueen needs, and adding the use case later does not fix it. Facebook's consent screen then fails with **Invalid Scopes**. If you see that, create a new app through **Other**.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the Business type">
    Select **Business** as the app type and choose **Next**. Connect your business portfolio now or later, then choose **Create app**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Facebook Login for Business">
    In the app dashboard, choose **Add Product**, find **Facebook Login for Business** and choose **Set up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the redirect URIs">
    Open **Facebook Login for Business > Settings**. Under **Client OAuth settings**, add the redirect URIs to **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs** and save.

    <OAuthRedirect />

    <CodeGroup>

    ```text Your domain
    https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/facebook
    https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/instagram
    ```

    ```text Local development
    http://localhost:4200/integrations/social/facebook
    http://localhost:4200/integrations/social/instagram
    ```

    ```text Docker on your machine
    http://localhost:4007/integrations/social/facebook
    http://localhost:4007/integrations/social/instagram
    ```

    </CodeGroup>

    Both tiles send `FRONTEND_URL` as it is, so the URIs stay plain, `http` included. Add the `/instagram` line only if you connect Instagram (Facebook Business).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the permissions">
    Open **App Review > Permissions and Features** and check that these are listed:

    - **Facebook Page**: `pages_show_list`, `business_management`, `pages_manage_posts`, `pages_manage_engagement`, `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_read_user_content` and `read_insights`.
    - **Instagram (Facebook Business)** adds `instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`, `instagram_manage_comments` and `instagram_manage_insights`.

    While the app is in development, people with a role on it can grant all of them.

    Do not add Instagram (Standalone) permissions (`instagram_business_*`) or Threads permissions (`threads_*`) to this app: they belong to separate apps. Do not request messaging, ads or `pages_manage_metadata` permissions either: PostQueen does not use them.

    <Note>
    Meta requires `pages_read_user_content` together with `pages_manage_engagement`, which PostQueen uses to publish follow-up comments under Page posts. PostQueen does not read other people's comments or posts.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the callback URLs">
    When someone removes your app on Facebook, or asks for their data to be deleted, Meta calls your server. PostQueen answers both calls: removing the app flags that person's channels for reconnection, and a deletion request deletes their channels and those channels' posts.

    - In **App settings > Basic**, under **User data deletion**, choose the data deletion callback URL and enter `NEXT_PUBLIC_BACKEND_URL` + `/integrations/facebook/platform-deletion`.
    - In **Facebook Login for Business > Settings**, set **Deauthorize callback URL** to `NEXT_PUBLIC_BACKEND_URL` + `/integrations/facebook/platform-deauthorize`.

    With the backend at `https://postqueen.example.com/api`, the first is `https://postqueen.example.com/api/integrations/facebook/platform-deletion`. One pair of URLs covers both tiles on this app. Meta only calls a public `https` address, so skip this step on a local install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the App ID and App secret">
    Open **App settings > Basic** and copy the **App ID** and the **App secret** (choose **Show**).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the variables

Add the two keys to your environment:

```env
FACEBOOK_APP_ID="your app id"
FACEBOOK_APP_SECRET="your app secret"
```

<RestartAndAddChannel />

Pick **Facebook Page**, log in with Facebook and share the Page. In **Select Page**, pick one or more Pages and choose **Save**. Pages you manage through Business Manager are listed too.

## App review

A new app starts in **Development** mode:

- Only people with a role on the app (administrator, developer or tester) can connect their Pages. Add people under **App roles > Roles**.
- Posts the app publishes are visible only to people with a role on the app until you switch it to **Live**. Everyone else sees nothing, or sees a post without its image.

To publish to Pages you own or manage, Standard Access is enough: switch **App Mode** to **Live** at the top of the dashboard. Meta asks for the display name, a contact email, a privacy policy URL and a terms of service URL in **App settings > Basic** first.

To let anyone else connect their Pages, request **Advanced Access** for each permission in **App Review > Permissions and Features**, and verify your business portfolio. Meta asks for a description and a screen recording of how the app uses each permission. Request only the permissions in step 6.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Invalid Scopes on Facebook's consent screen">
    The app was created through a use case. Create a new app through **Other**, as in step 2, and move the keys over.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="URL Blocked on Facebook's login screen">
    The redirect URI PostQueen sent is not in **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs**. Add it exactly as in step 5, with the scheme and port your `FRONTEND_URL` uses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not enough scopes, when choosing a provider, please add all the scopes">
    A permission was declined on the consent screen, or is missing from the app. Check the list in step 6, then connect again and approve everything.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="PostQueen is not authorized to publish as this page, please reconnect the channel">
    The Page was not shared during the login. Reconnect the channel and include the Page when Facebook asks which Pages to share.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Posts show for you but not for anyone else">
    The app is still in **Development** mode. Switch **App Mode** to **Live**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
