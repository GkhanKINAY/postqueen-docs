On your own instance the TikTok app is yours: register it on TikTok for Developers, then put its client key and secret in your environment.

PostQueen has two TikTok cards in **Add Channel**, and each needs its own app:

| Card | TikTok API | Keys | Redirect URI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **TikTok** | Login Kit and the Content Posting API ([developers.tiktok.com](https://developers.tiktok.com/apps)) | `TIKTOK_CLIENT_ID`, `TIKTOK_CLIENT_SECRET` | `/integrations/social/tiktok`, **no** trailing slash |
| **TikTok Business** | TikTok's Business API (Accounts API) | `TIKTOK_BUSINESS_CLIENT_ID`, `TIKTOK_BUSINESS_CLIENT_SECRET` | `/integrations/social/tiktok-business/`, **with** a trailing slash |

Set up the standard **TikTok** app first. The Business card is optional and is covered at the end of this page.

## Two gates before posts are public

TikTok reviews apps twice, and the two are easy to confuse:

1. **App review** moves the app from Sandbox to Production (**Live**). Before that, only the target users of a sandbox can connect.
2. **The Content Posting API audit** is what allows public posts. Until an app passes it, TikTok accepts only the **Self only** privacy level, and only a few users of the app can post per 24 hours. Being Live does not lift this.

Portal reference: [Register your app](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/getting-started-create-an-app), [App review guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/app-review-guidelines), [Content sharing guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines).

## Scopes (exactly six)

PostQueen compares the scopes TikTok grants with the six it asks for and refuses the connect when any is missing, with `Not enough scopes, when choosing a provider, please add all the scopes`. Enable these six and nothing else:

| Scope | What PostQueen uses it for |
| --- | --- |
| `user.info.basic` | The account id, avatar and display name on the channel |
| `user.info.profile` | The `@username` and profile link |
| `user.info.stats` | Follower, following, like and video counts on Analytics |
| `video.list` | Your videos and their view, like, comment and share counts |
| `video.upload` | **Upload** mode: the media lands in the TikTok app's inbox for the user to finish there |
| `video.publish` | **Direct post**: PostQueen publishes the post itself |

Every extra scope has to be shown in your review video, so adding more only slows the review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the app">
    [Sign up](https://developers.tiktok.com/signup) for a developer account if you do not have one, open [Manage apps](https://developers.tiktok.com/apps) and click **Connect an app**. TikTok lets you register the app under an organization, which is the better owner for an app your team keeps.

    Fill in the app details:

    | Field | What to enter |
    | --- | --- |
    | App name | Your product's name. Do not put "TikTok" in it |
    | Platforms | **Web** |
    | Website | Your public website, not a login-only page |
    | Terms of Service, Privacy Policy | Links that are visible on that website without opening a menu |
    | App icon | Your own icon, without TikTok's logo |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Login Kit and the Content Posting API">
    In **Add products**, add:

    1. **Login Kit**, platform **Web**, with the redirect URI below.
    2. **Content Posting API**, with **Direct Post** turned on. PostQueen publishes directly by default; Upload mode is the exception.

    Nothing else is needed. Share Kit, Webhooks, Data Portability and the Research API are not used by PostQueen.

    <Snippet file="oauth2redirect.mdx" />

    **Your TikTok redirect URI:**
    - Production: `https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/tiktok`
    - Local development: `https://redirectmeto.com/http://localhost:4200/integrations/social/tiktok`
    - Docker: `https://redirectmeto.com/http://localhost:4007/integrations/social/tiktok`

    <Warning>
    TikTok only accepts `https` redirect URIs. When your `FRONTEND_URL` is plain `http`, PostQueen wraps the callback through `redirectmeto.com`, so that wrapped address is the one TikTok receives and the one you register. Compare it character for character: no trailing slash, no query string.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable the six scopes">
    Enable the six scopes from the table above and save.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your media domain">
    TikTok downloads **photos** from their URL (`PULL_FROM_URL`), and it only downloads from URL properties you have verified. Open **URL properties** in the portal and verify the domain your uploads are served from (with local storage that is your `FRONTEND_URL` host; with [Cloudflare R2](/configuration/r2) it is your public bucket domain). An unverified domain fails the post with TikTok's `url_ownership_unverified` error.

    **Videos** on the TikTok card are uploaded by PostQueen itself (`FILE_UPLOAD`, in 10 MB chunks above 64 MB), so they do not depend on this step.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test in a sandbox">
    Switch the portal toggle to **Sandbox** and create a sandbox. A sandbox needs no review; you can have up to 5 per app and up to 10 **target users** in each. Add the TikTok account you will connect as a target user.

    Copy the **client key and secret shown while Sandbox is selected** (they usually differ from Production) into your environment:

```env
TIKTOK_CLIENT_ID="your-client-key"
TIKTOK_CLIENT_SECRET="your-client-secret"
```

    There are no separate sandbox variables. When the app goes Live, put the Production pair in the same two variables.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Restart and connect">
    <Snippet file="restart-and-add-channel.mdx" />

    Pick the **TikTok** card, not TikTok Business, and approve all six scopes.

    Until the audit, choose **Self only** as the privacy level in the post settings. Any other level is refused with: *This TikTok app has not passed TikTok's review yet, so it can only publish with the "Self only" privacy level.*
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Switch to **Production** and submit the app for review. TikTok asks for at least one demo video (up to 5, 50 MB each) of the full flow, and an app that has not been approved before has to record that demo in a sandbox. Show the connect, the consent screen with the six scopes, and a post going out.

    After the app is Live, any TikTok user can connect it, still limited to **Self only** until the Content Posting API audit passes. When TikTok's daily cap of posting users for an unaudited app is reached, PostQueen disconnects the channel with *TikTok daily user limit reached, please re-connect your account*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
The channel shows up with its avatar and name. That is the whole confirmation for the connect; whether a post can be public depends on the audit above.
</Check>

## TikTok Business (optional)

The **TikTok Business** card talks to TikTok's Business API instead of the Content Posting API. It is a separate app with separate keys, registered for the Accounts API on the [TikTok API for Business](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal) developer portal.

- **Redirect URI, with the trailing slash:** `https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/tiktok-business/`. PostQueen sends it with the slash on both the login and the token exchange, so a URI registered without it fails. The `redirectmeto.com` wrap applies here too when `FRONTEND_URL` is `http`.
- **Scopes** (all six are checked at connect): `user.info.basic`, `user.info.username`, `user.info.stats`, `video.list`, `video.upload`, `video.publish`.
- **Media:** the Business API pulls **both photos and videos** from their URL, so the media domain must be a verified URL property of this app as well.

```env
TIKTOK_BUSINESS_CLIENT_ID="your-business-client-id"
TIKTOK_BUSINESS_CLIENT_SECRET="your-business-client-secret"
```

<Warning>
In the web editor, the privacy picker loads its options from TikTok through a call only the standard TikTok card implements, so a Direct post on a TikTok Business channel cannot get its privacy level from the editor. Posts sent through the API, the CLI or MCP pass `privacy_level` themselves.
</Warning>

### Moving channels from TikTok to TikTok Business

`MIGRATE_PROVIDERS` sends a reconnect of the old card through the new card's login and keeps the channel, its scheduled posts and its settings; `HIDDEN_PROVIDERS` takes the old card out of **Add Channel**:

```env
MIGRATE_PROVIDERS="tiktok:tiktok-business"
HIDDEN_PROVIDERS="tiktok"
```

Both are described in the [configuration reference](/configuration/reference).

## Common mistakes

- A trailing slash on the standard TikTok redirect, or a missing one on the Business redirect.
- Registering the bare `http://localhost` URI while `FRONTEND_URL` is `http`: register the `redirectmeto.com` form.
- Enabling scopes beyond the six, or missing one of them.
- Using the Production key while the app is only testable in Sandbox, or the reverse.
- Expecting public posts after the app goes Live, before the Content Posting API audit.
