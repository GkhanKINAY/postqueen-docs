On a self-hosted PostQueen the TikTok app is yours. **Add Channel** has two TikTok tiles, and each needs its own app: **TikTok** uses Login Kit and the Content Posting API on TikTok for Developers, and **TikTok Business** uses TikTok's Business API. Set up **TikTok** first. TikTok Business is optional and covered at the end of this page.

## Before you start

| | |
|---|---|
| Developer console | [TikTok for Developers](https://developers.tiktok.com/apps), with a developer account |
| Variables | `TIKTOK_CLIENT_ID` (the client key), `TIKTOK_CLIENT_SECRET` |
| Redirect URI | `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/tiktok`, with no trailing slash. When `FRONTEND_URL` is plain `http`, PostQueen sends it wrapped as `https://redirectmeto.com/` + that address |
| Products | **Login Kit** (Web) and the **Content Posting API** with **Direct Post** turned on |
| Scopes | `user.info.basic`, `user.info.profile`, `user.info.stats`, `video.list`, `video.upload`, `video.publish` |
| Review | App review to take the app live, then TikTok's Content Posting API audit before posts can be anything but private |

PostQueen checks all six scopes when an account connects. A missing one stops the connect.

## Create the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect an app">
    Sign in to [TikTok for Developers](https://developers.tiktok.com/), open your profile menu and choose **Manage apps**, then **Connect an app**. In **Select the app owner**, pick your organization or your own account and choose **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the app details">
    In **App details**, enter the **App icon** (1024 x 1024 px), **App name**, **Category** and **Description**, plus your **Terms of Service URL** and **Privacy Policy URL**. Under **Platforms**, select **Web** and enter your website. TikTok shows the name and description on its authorization screen, and reviews all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Login Kit">
    In **Products**, choose **Add products** and add **Login Kit**. In its settings, add the **Redirect URI** for the **Web** platform.

    <OAuthRedirect />

    | Where PostQueen runs | Redirect URI |
    |---|---|
    | Your domain | `https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/tiktok` |
    | Local development | `https://redirectmeto.com/http://localhost:4200/integrations/social/tiktok` |
    | Docker on your machine | `https://redirectmeto.com/http://localhost:4007/integrations/social/tiktok` |

    TikTok accepts only `https` redirect URIs with no query string. That is why PostQueen wraps a plain `http` address through `redirectmeto.com`: register the wrapped address, character for character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Content Posting API">
    Choose **Add products** again and add the **Content Posting API**. Turn on **Direct Post**: PostQueen publishes directly by default, and uses the inbox upload only when a post asks for it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the six scopes">
    In **Scopes**, add `user.info.basic`, `user.info.profile`, `user.info.stats`, `video.list`, `video.upload` and `video.publish`. Add no others: each scope you request has to be shown in your review video.

    | Scope | What PostQueen uses it for |
    |---|---|
    | `user.info.basic` | The account's id, avatar and display name |
    | `user.info.profile` | The account's username |
    | `user.info.stats` | Follower, following, like and video counts for analytics |
    | `video.list` | Your recent videos and their view, like, comment and share counts |
    | `video.upload` | Sending a post to the account's TikTok inbox |
    | `video.publish` | Publishing a post directly |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the domain your media comes from">
    TikTok fetches photos from their URL, and only from domains you verified. Choose **URL properties** at the top of the app page, make sure **Production** is selected, choose **Verify properties** and verify by **Domain** or **URL prefix**. Verify the host your uploads are served from: your `FRONTEND_URL` host with local storage, or your public bucket's domain with [Cloudflare R2](/configuration/r2).

    Videos on the **TikTok** tile are uploaded by PostQueen itself, so they do not depend on this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the client key and secret">
    The **Client key** and **Client secret** are under **Credentials** in **App details**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the variables

Add the two keys to your environment:

```env
TIKTOK_CLIENT_ID="your client key"
TIKTOK_CLIENT_SECRET="your client secret"
```

<RestartAndAddChannel />

Pick **TikTok**, not **TikTok Business**, and approve every permission.

## App review

TikTok has two separate gates, and passing the first does not open the second.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Test in a sandbox">
    Before review, only a sandbox can connect. Switch the toggle next to the app's name to **Sandbox**, choose **Create Sandbox**, and add the TikTok accounts you will connect as target users. An app can have up to 5 sandboxes with up to 10 target users each. Use the client key and secret shown while **Sandbox** is selected, and switch to the **Production** pair once the app is live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the app for review">
    Switch to **Production**, complete the **App review** section and upload 1 to 5 demo videos (up to 50 MB each) showing the connect, the consent screen with the six scopes, and a post going out. Choose **Submit for review**. The status under **Production** moves from **Draft** to **In review**, then to **Live** or **Not approved**. Once the app is live, any TikTok account can connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass the Content Posting API audit">
    Until your app passes TikTok's [Content Posting API audit](https://developers.tiktok.com/application/content-posting-api), TikTok publishes every post as private, and at most 5 people can post through your app in any 24 hours. Being live does not lift this. Submit the audit once your integration works.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until the audit passes, choose **Self only** as the audience in each post. Any other audience is refused at publish time with: "This TikTok app has not passed TikTok's review yet, so it can only publish with the "Self only" privacy level. Choose Self only, or submit the app for review in the TikTok developer portal."

## TikTok Business (optional)

The **TikTok Business** tile talks to TikTok's Business API instead of the Content Posting API. It needs a separate app, registered on the [TikTok API for Business](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal) developer portal, with its own keys:

| | |
|---|---|
| Variables | `TIKTOK_BUSINESS_CLIENT_ID`, `TIKTOK_BUSINESS_CLIENT_SECRET` |
| Redirect URI | `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/tiktok-business/`, **with** a trailing slash. PostQueen sends the slash on both the login and the token exchange, so a URI registered without it fails. The `redirectmeto.com` wrap applies here too |
| Scopes | `user.info.basic`, `user.info.username`, `user.info.stats`, `video.list`, `video.upload`, `video.publish`, all checked at connect |
| Media | The Business API fetches photos and videos from their URL, so your media domain must be a verified URL property of this app too |

```env
TIKTOK_BUSINESS_CLIENT_ID="your business client id"
TIKTOK_BUSINESS_CLIENT_SECRET="your business client secret"
```

<Warning>
In the web editor, the audience picker is loaded through a call only the **TikTok** tile implements, so a direct post on a **TikTok Business** channel cannot get its audience from the editor. Posts sent through the API or an AI agent set `privacy_level` themselves.
</Warning>

To move existing channels from one tile to the other, `MIGRATE_PROVIDERS="tiktok:tiktok-business"` sends a reconnect of the old tile through the new tile's login and keeps the channel and its posts, and `HIDDEN_PROVIDERS="tiktok"` takes the old tile out of **Add Channel**. Both are in the [configuration reference](/configuration/reference).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="redirect_uri error on TikTok's login screen">
    The redirect URI PostQueen sent is not the one registered in Login Kit. Check the trailing slash (none on **TikTok**, one on **TikTok Business**) and, when `FRONTEND_URL` is `http`, register the `redirectmeto.com` form from step 3.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not enough scopes, when choosing a provider, please add all the scopes">
    One of the six scopes is missing from the app or was declined on the consent screen. Check the list in step 5, then connect again and approve everything.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="TikTok daily user limit reached, please re-connect your account">
    More than 5 people posted through your unaudited app in 24 hours, and TikTok disconnected the channel. Reconnect it later, or pass the Content Posting API audit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You have to upload the picture/video to PostQueen when sending a URL">
    TikTok could not fetch the photo because its domain is not a verified URL property of the app. Verify the domain your uploads are served from, as in step 6.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An account that is not a sandbox target user cannot connect">
    The app is not live yet. Add the account as a target user of the sandbox, or submit the app for review.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
