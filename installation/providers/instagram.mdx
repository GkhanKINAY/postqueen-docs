Instagram has two tiles in **Add Channel**, and each logs in through a different Meta app. Set up one or both.

| | Instagram (Facebook Business) | Instagram (Standalone) |
|---|---|---|
| Login | Facebook Login for Business | Instagram Login |
| Meta app | The [Facebook app](/installation/providers/facebook), shared with Facebook Page | A separate app of its own |
| Variables | `FACEBOOK_APP_ID`, `FACEBOOK_APP_SECRET` | `INSTAGRAM_APP_ID`, `INSTAGRAM_APP_SECRET` |
| Redirect URI | `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/instagram` | `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/instagram-standalone`, `https` only |
| Permissions | `instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`, `instagram_manage_comments`, `instagram_manage_insights`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `business_management` | `instagram_business_basic`, `instagram_business_content_publish`, `instagram_business_manage_comments`, `instagram_business_manage_insights` |
| Account needed | Business or Creator, linked to a Facebook Page | Business or Creator, no Page needed |
| Music on Reels | Yes | No |

<Warning>
Meta does not allow Facebook Login and Instagram Login on the same app. Never add **Facebook Login for Business** to the Standalone app, and never reuse the Facebook app for Standalone.
</Warning>

PostQueen checks every permission it asks for when an account connects. A missing one stops the connect.

## Instagram (Facebook Business)

This tile uses the Facebook app. Create it with [Facebook setup](/installation/providers/facebook), then make sure of three things on that app:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the Instagram redirect URI">
    In **Facebook Login for Business > Settings**, **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs** lists `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/instagram`, for example `https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/instagram`. The tile sends `FRONTEND_URL` as it is, so the URI stays plain, `http` included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Instagram permissions">
    **App Review > Permissions and Features** lists `instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`, `instagram_manage_comments` and `instagram_manage_insights`, next to the Page permissions the Facebook setup added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the channel">
    With `FACEBOOK_APP_ID` and `FACEBOOK_APP_SECRET` set, pick **Instagram (Facebook Business)** in **Add Channel**, log in with Facebook and share the Page linked to the Instagram account. In **Select Instagram Account**, pick the accounts and choose **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The callback URLs set on the Facebook app cover this tile too.

## Instagram (Standalone)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a separate app">
    Open [Create an app](https://developers.facebook.com/apps/creation/) in Meta for Developers. Enter the app name and a contact email, choose **Other** as the use case, then **Business** as the app type, and choose **Create app**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Instagram product">
    In the app dashboard, find **Instagram** and choose **Set up**. Meta adds **API setup with Instagram login**. Do not switch to the Facebook login setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Instagram app ID and secret">
    Open **Instagram > API setup with Instagram login** and copy the **Instagram app ID** and the **Instagram app secret** (choose **Show**). These are not the App ID and App secret under **App settings > Basic**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up Instagram business login">
    In **3. Set up Instagram business login**, choose **Set up** and enter the redirect URL, then save. Meta compares it character for character, so check it has no trailing slash added.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/providers/instagram-standalone/instagram-standalone-003.png" alt="The Set up Instagram business login dialog in Meta's app dashboard, with the Redirect URL field" />
    </Frame>

    <OAuthRedirect />

    With `FRONTEND_URL` set to `https://postqueen.example.com`, the redirect URL is:

    ```text
    https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/instagram-standalone
    ```

    Instagram Login accepts only `https` redirects. When `FRONTEND_URL` is plain `http`, PostQueen sends the callback through `https://redirectmeto.com/` first, and that wrapped address is the one to register. For local development, and for Docker on port 4007:

    ```text
    https://redirectmeto.com/http://localhost:4200/integrations/social/instagram-standalone
    https://redirectmeto.com/http://localhost:4007/integrations/social/instagram-standalone
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the callback URLs">
    In the same **Business login settings**, set:

    - **Deauthorize callback URL**: `NEXT_PUBLIC_BACKEND_URL` + `/integrations/instagram-standalone/platform-deauthorize`
    - **Data deletion request URL**: `NEXT_PUBLIC_BACKEND_URL` + `/integrations/instagram-standalone/platform-deletion`

    With the backend at `https://postqueen.example.com/api`, the first is `https://postqueen.example.com/api/integrations/instagram-standalone/platform-deauthorize`. Removing the app on Instagram flags that account's channels for reconnection, and a deletion request deletes them and their posts. Meta only calls a public `https` address, so skip this step on a local install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the permissions">
    PostQueen asks for exactly four: `instagram_business_basic`, `instagram_business_content_publish`, `instagram_business_manage_comments` and `instagram_business_manage_insights`. Do not add `instagram_business_manage_messages` or any `pages_*` permission to this app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Instagram accounts that will connect">
    While the app is in development, only accounts with a role on it can connect. In **1. Generate access tokens**, choose **Add account** and sign in with each Instagram account, or invite them as **Instagram Tester** under **App roles > Roles**. An invited account accepts in Instagram, under [Apps and websites](https://www.instagram.com/accounts/manage_access/).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the variables

Add the keys for the tiles you set up:

```env
# Instagram (Facebook Business) and Facebook Page, one shared app
FACEBOOK_APP_ID="your facebook app id"
FACEBOOK_APP_SECRET="your facebook app secret"

# Instagram (Standalone), its own app
INSTAGRAM_APP_ID="your instagram app id"
INSTAGRAM_APP_SECRET="your instagram app secret"
```

<RestartAndAddChannel />

Pick **Instagram (Standalone)** or **Instagram (Facebook Business)**, and approve every permission on Meta's screen.

## App review

Both apps start in **Development** mode. Until they are reviewed, only accounts with a role on the app can connect, and posts made in development are visible only to those accounts.

- **Your own accounts.** Standard Access is enough for accounts you own or manage. Switch **App Mode** to **Live** so that everyone can see the posts. Meta asks for the display name, a contact email, a privacy policy URL and a terms of service URL in **App settings > Basic** first.
- **Other people's accounts.** Request **Advanced Access** for each permission and verify your business portfolio. On the Standalone app, start from **4. Complete app review** in **Instagram > API setup with Instagram login**; on the Facebook app, use **App Review > Permissions and Features**. Meta asks for a description and a screen recording of how the app uses each permission, and for at least one successful API call per permission. Request only the permissions in the table above.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Insufficient developer role">
    The Instagram account has no role on the Standalone app. Add it as in the last Standalone step, accept the invitation in Instagram, and connect again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invalid redirect_uri on Instagram's login screen">
    The redirect URL PostQueen sent is not registered, or differs by a trailing slash. On a plain `http` install, register the `redirectmeto.com` address from the table, not the bare `localhost` one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not enough scopes, when choosing a provider, please add all the scopes">
    A permission was declined on Meta's screen, or is missing from the app. Check the permissions in the table at the top, then connect again and approve everything.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invalid Scopes on Facebook's consent screen">
    The Facebook app was created through a use case. See [Facebook setup](/installation/providers/facebook): create the app through **Other**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="We couldn't find any business connected to the selected pages.">
    On the Facebook Business tile, no Page shared during the login has an Instagram account linked. Link the Instagram account to a Facebook Page, then connect again and share that Page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
