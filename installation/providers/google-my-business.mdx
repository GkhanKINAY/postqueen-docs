On a self-hosted PostQueen the Google Cloud project is yours. Google must first approve the project for the Business Profile APIs. Then you turn on four APIs, create a **Web application** OAuth client, and give PostQueen its client ID and secret. In **Add Channel** the tile is named **Google My Business**.

## Before you start

| | |
|---|---|
| Developer console | [Google Cloud console](https://console.cloud.google.com/), with a Google account that owns or manages the Business Profile |
| Variables | `GOOGLE_GMB_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_GMB_CLIENT_SECRET`. Either one left empty is replaced by `YOUTUBE_CLIENT_ID` or `YOUTUBE_CLIENT_SECRET` |
| Redirect URI | `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/gmb` |
| APIs | **Google My Business API**, **My Business Account Management API**, **My Business Business Information API** and **Business Profile Performance API** |
| Scopes | `userinfo.profile`, `userinfo.email`, `business.manage` |
| You also need | A Business Profile that has been verified and active for at least 60 days, and a website listed on it |
| Review | Google's approval of Business Profile API access for your project. See Request API access |

PostQueen checks all three scopes when a channel connects. A missing one stops the connect.

## Request API access

Until Google approves your project, its Business Profile API quota is 0 requests a minute, and nothing can be read or posted.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the project">
    In the [Google Cloud console](https://console.cloud.google.com/), choose **Create project**, enter a name and choose **Create**. Copy the **Project number** from the **Project info** card on the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the access request">
    Open Google's [Business Profile API contact form](https://support.google.com/business/contact/api_default) and choose **Application for Basic API Access**. Enter the project number, and use an email address that is an owner or manager of the Business Profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the approval">
    Google reviews requests within 14 days. Once approved, the quota of the Business Profile APIs in your project shows 300 requests a minute instead of 0.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create the client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the four APIs">
    Open **APIs and services > Library** and enable each of these:

    - **Google My Business API**: publishes the posts. Google shows it only to projects whose access request was approved.
    - **My Business Account Management API**: lists your Business Profile accounts when you connect.
    - **My Business Business Information API**: lists the locations in them.
    - **Business Profile Performance API**: the numbers on the Analytics page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up Branding and Audience">
    Open [Google Auth Platform](https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/overview). In **Branding**, enter the app name, a support email, your home page and privacy policy links, and your domain under **Authorized domains**. In **Audience**, choose **External**, unless everyone who will connect belongs to your Google Workspace, where **Internal** skips verification.

    While the app is in **Testing**, only the accounts under **Test users** can connect. Add yours with **Add users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the scopes">
    In **Data access**, choose **Add or remove scopes**, add the three scopes and save:

    ```text
    https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile
    https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email
    https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Web application client">
    In **Clients**, choose **Create client** and pick **Web application**. Under **Authorized redirect URIs**, add:

    <CodeGroup>

    ```text Your domain
    https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/gmb
    ```

    ```text Local development
    http://localhost:4200/integrations/social/gmb
    ```

    ```text Docker on your machine
    http://localhost:4007/integrations/social/gmb
    ```

    </CodeGroup>

    Google accepts only `https`, except for `localhost`.

    <OAuthRedirect />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the client ID and secret">
    Choose **Create** and copy the **Client ID** and **Client secret** from the dialog. Google shows the secret only now. If you lose it, add a new secret to the same client.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the variables

Add the two keys to your environment:

```env
GOOGLE_GMB_CLIENT_ID="your client id"
GOOGLE_GMB_CLIENT_SECRET="your client secret"
```

<Note>
**Sharing the YouTube client.** If you leave both variables empty, PostQueen uses the YouTube client from [YouTube setup](/installation/providers/youtube) instead. That client then needs the `/integrations/social/gmb` redirect URI, the `business.manage` scope, and the four APIs in its project, and the project needs the Business Profile API approval. Set both variables or neither: each empty one is replaced on its own, so setting only one mixes two clients.
</Note>

Pick **Google My Business**, sign in with Google and approve every permission, then choose the location. Every location in every account that Google user manages is listed. Unverified locations do not accept posts.

## App review

- **Testing.** Google gives an External app in **Testing** refresh tokens that expire after 7 days, so a channel connected then has to be reconnected every week. In **Audience**, choose **Publish app** to leave Testing.
- **Verification.** Once published, people who connect see Google's unverified-app screen until Google verifies the app in the **Verification Center**, and an unverified app that uses sensitive scopes is limited to 100 users. An install used only by you and your team can stay unverified.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Google My Business API is not in the API library">
    Google shows it only once your access request is approved. Check the email address you used on the request form, or send the form again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rate limit exceeded. Please try again later.">
    Google refused the request for quota. On a new project, the Business Profile quota is still 0 because the access request is not approved yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Permission denied. Please ensure you have access to this business location.">
    The Google account is not an owner or manager of the location, or an API is not enabled. Check the four APIs in step 1, and the account's role on the Business Profile. A Google Workspace account also needs Business Profile turned on by its admin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not enough scopes, when choosing a provider, please add all the scopes">
    A scope is missing from **Data access**, or was declined on the consent screen. Add all three, then connect again and approve everything.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="redirect_uri_mismatch on Google's screen">
    The redirect URI PostQueen sent is not on the client it used. It is exactly your `FRONTEND_URL` followed by `/integrations/social/gmb`. If `GOOGLE_GMB_CLIENT_ID` is empty, add it to the YouTube client instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
