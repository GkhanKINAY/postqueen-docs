On a self-hosted PostQueen the VK ID app is yours. You create a **Web** app in the VK ID business account, add PostQueen's trusted redirect URL, and give PostQueen the app ID. VK ID signs in with PKCE, so there is no secret to set. Posting to a wall also needs an access right that VK grants only on request.

## Before you start

| | |
|---|---|
| Developer portal | [VK ID for business](https://id.vk.com/about/business/go), signed in with VK Business ID. The console is in Russian |
| Variable | `VK_ID` |
| Redirect URI | `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/vk` when it starts with `https`. Otherwise `https://redirectmeto.com/` + `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/vk` |
| Scopes | `vkid.personal_info`, `email`, `wall`, `status`, `docs`, `photos`, `video` |
| Review | VK's approval of the extended access rights, such as `wall`, `photos` and `video`, and verification of your business profile. See Access rights |

PostQueen does not check the granted scopes when a channel connects.

## Create the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the application">
    In the VK ID business account, open **Мои приложения** (My apps) and choose **Добавить приложение** (Add application). Enter a name, choose the **Web** platform and pick an image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the base domain and trusted redirect URL">
    Fill in **Базовый домен** (base domain) and **Доверенный redirect URL** (trusted redirect URL). The redirect URL's domain must match the base domain exactly.

    VK does not send people back to a plain `http` address, so when your `FRONTEND_URL` is not `https`, PostQueen sends VK a callback wrapped in `redirectmeto.com`, and that wrapped address is the one to register:

    | How you run PostQueen | Base domain | Trusted redirect URL |
    |---|---|---|
    | Your domain | `postqueen.example.com` | `https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/vk` |
    | Local development | `redirectmeto.com` | `https://redirectmeto.com/http://localhost:4200/integrations/social/vk` |
    | Docker on your machine | `redirectmeto.com` | `https://redirectmeto.com/http://localhost:4007/integrations/social/vk` |

    Choose **Создать приложение** (Create application).

    <OAuthRedirect />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on email">
    Under **Доступы** (Access), switch on **Почта** (email) and save. PostQueen asks for the `email` scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the app ID">
    Open the app card, then **Информация о приложении** (App information), and copy **ID приложения** (App ID). That is the only value PostQueen needs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the variable

Add the app ID to your environment:

```env
VK_ID="your app id"
```

PostQueen sends it on the sign-in, on the token exchange and on every wall call, so it stays in use for the life of the channel.

<RestartAndAddChannel />

Pick **VK**, sign in with VK ID and approve access. Posts go to that account's own wall.

## Access rights

- **Extended rights.** VK ID grants `vkid.personal_info`, `email` and `phone` to any app. The others PostQueen asks for, `wall`, `photos`, `video`, `docs` and `status`, are extended rights that VK grants only in exceptional cases. Request them by email to `devsupport@corp.vk.com`, saying what your app posts and why. Until VK grants them, the sign-in can stop with `invalid_scope`.
- **Business verification.** VK asks accounts created after December 15, 2025 to verify their business profile within 60 days of their first app, or the apps are blocked.
- **The code exchange.** PostQueen sends no secret or service key when it exchanges the sign-in code. If VK refuses the exchange, check the confidentiality level under **Ключи доступа** (Access keys): a confidential app expects a service key.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="VK stops with invalid_scope">
    The app does not have every right PostQueen asks for. Request the extended rights as described in Access rights, then connect again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="VK says the redirect URL does not match">
    The trusted redirect URL is not exactly the one PostQueen sent. With a plain `http` `FRONTEND_URL`, register the `redirectmeto.com` address from step 2, not the bare `localhost` one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A post shows as published, but it is not on the wall">
    VK reports some errors inside a normal-looking answer, and PostQueen does not read them, so a post made without the `wall` right can show as published. Check that VK granted the app the `wall` right.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
