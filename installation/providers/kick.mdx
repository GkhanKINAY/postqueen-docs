On a self-hosted PostQueen the Kick app is yours. You create it in your Kick developer settings and give PostQueen its client ID and secret. Kick does not review apps.

## Before you start

| | |
|---|---|
| Developer portal | [Kick developer settings](https://kick.com/settings/developer), with two-factor authentication on your Kick account |
| Variables | `KICK_CLIENT_ID`, `KICK_SECRET` |
| Redirect URI | `FRONTEND_URL` + `/integrations/social/kick` |
| Scopes | `chat:write`, `user:read`, `channel:read` |
| Review | None |

PostQueen signs in with OAuth 2.1 and PKCE on `id.kick.com`, and sends the secret as well. It does not check which scopes were granted.

## Create the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on two-factor authentication">
    Kick's developer tools need two-factor authentication on your account. Turn it on in your Kick security settings, then open **Settings > Developer** at [kick.com/settings/developer](https://kick.com/settings/developer).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the app">
    Create a new app and fill in its name, description and redirect URL. Leave webhooks off: PostQueen does not use them. Tick the three scopes PostQueen asks for: reading user information (`user:read`), reading channel information (`channel:read`) and writing to chat (`chat:write`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the redirect URL">
    Use the address that matches how you run PostQueen:

    <CodeGroup>

    ```text Your domain
    https://postqueen.example.com/integrations/social/kick
    ```

    ```text Local development
    http://localhost:4200/integrations/social/kick
    ```

    ```text Docker on your machine
    http://localhost:4007/integrations/social/kick
    ```

    </CodeGroup>

    Kick compares it exactly. Use `localhost`, not `127.0.0.1`: Kick documents that sign-ins through `127.0.0.1` break.

    <OAuthRedirect />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the client ID and secret">
    Your developer settings show the app's **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. Copy both, and keep the secret private.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the variables

Add the two keys to your environment:

```env
KICK_CLIENT_ID="your client id"
KICK_SECRET="your client secret"
```

<Warning>
The second variable is `KICK_SECRET`, not `KICK_CLIENT_SECRET`. The long name is ignored, and the sign-in then fails at the token exchange.
</Warning>

<RestartAndAddChannel />

Pick **Kick**, sign in, and approve the chat permission. Posts go to the chat of the channel you signed in with.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Kick shows a bare 404 page when you connect">
    Kick shows that page when the sign-in request is missing something it needs. Check `KICK_CLIENT_ID`, and that the redirect URL on the app is exactly your `FRONTEND_URL` followed by `/integrations/social/kick`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The sign-in comes back, but the channel is not added">
    The token exchange failed. The usual cause is the secret: check that it is in `KICK_SECRET` and is the app's current secret.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Messages do not appear in chat">
    The token lacks `chat:write`. Tick it on the app, then reconnect the channel and approve every permission.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
