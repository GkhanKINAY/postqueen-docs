**Where:** [app.postqueen.ai](https://app.postqueen.ai). You need an email address and a card.

Three steps take you from a new account to a post on the calendar. PostQueen publishes it at the time you set, whether your computer is on or not.

## 1. Start the free trial

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Open [app.postqueen.ai](https://app.postqueen.ai) and fill in **Email**, **Password** and **Organization** under **Create your account**, or sign up with one of the sign-in buttons. Choose **Sign up**. If PostQueen asks you to activate your account, open the link in the email it sends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Under **Choose a Plan**, pick Creator, Growth, Pro or Ultimate, and **Monthly** or **Yearly**. [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans) compares them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a card and start">
    Enter your card and billing address, then choose **Pay $0 Today – Start your free trial!** Nothing is charged for {{trialDays}} days, and you can cancel before then.
  </Step>
</Steps>

[Free trial](/cloud/trial) explains the card check, what happens on day {{trialDays}} and how to cancel before it.

## 2. Connect your first channel

A channel is one account on a network, such as a Bluesky profile or a WordPress site. Not every network can be connected yet: [Channels and their status](/channels/overview) shows which are **Available**, **In review** or **Soon**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add Channel">
    Choose **Channels** in the left rail, then **Add Channel** at the top of the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the network">
    Choose its tile. Networks that use their own sign-in page show **Continue to** followed by the network's name; networks that need a key or an app password, such as Bluesky, show **Enter details**. The pane explains what to fill in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and check">
    Sign in on the network's page, or fill in the form. You come back to **Channels** with the new channel selected and marked **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

[Connect a channel](/using/connect-a-channel) covers reconnecting, disabling, deleting and invite links.

## 3. Schedule your first post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the composer">
    Choose **Create Post** at the top of any page. It opens on the first free quarter hour, usually one to four hours from now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the channels">
    Under **Select Channels**, click the avatars of the channels the post is for. Until you pick one, the main button reads **Select Channels** and stays disabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the post">
    Type in the editor. It goes to every channel you picked. Add images or a video from the buttons under the editor if you want them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the time and schedule">
    Change the date and time if you need to, then choose **Schedule**. A message confirms the time. **Save as draft** keeps the post without scheduling it, and **Post now** sits in the menu next to **Schedule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it went out

Open **Calendar** in the left rail: the post sits at its time. Once it is published, it moves to **Posted** in the posts panel, and **Go to post** on the post opens it on the network. If it failed, the post says so, and [Why a post failed](/troubleshooting/post-failed) explains what to do.
