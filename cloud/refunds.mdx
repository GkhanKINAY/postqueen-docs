**Where:** by email to [support@postqueen.ai](mailto:support@postqueen.ai), or through the [contact page](https://postqueen.ai/contact). There is no refund button in the app.

Two rules can give you money back: PostQueen's refund rule, which depends on when your account was created, and the right of withdrawal for consumers in the EU, the EEA and the UK, which applies to every account. Sections 9 and 10 of the [Terms of Service](https://postqueen.ai/terms-of-service) are the full text.

## What PostQueen refunds

For accounts created from 22 October 2026:

- **One month per account, at most.** A refund is limited to one month's plan fee per account.
- **Only after a recent charge.** PostQueen refunds only if the account's last charge was made within the {{refundWindowDays}} days before you ask.
- **Not yearly charges.** A yearly charge is not refunded under this rule. The [right of withdrawal](#right-of-withdrawal) and your other rights under the Terms and the law still apply.
- **Anything more is not guaranteed.** PostQueen may refund more than this rule gives, at its own discretion.
- **Credit packs and the founding member fee** are refunded only under the right of withdrawal, the other sections of the Terms or the law.

Accounts created before 22 October 2026 keep the earlier promise: any charge is refunded in full if you ask within 30 days of it. The [earlier Terms of Service](https://postqueen.ai/terms-of-service/2026-10-21) still apply to those accounts.

## Right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you can withdraw within {{withdrawalDays}} days without giving a reason and get back everything you paid. Nothing is deducted for the days you used PostQueen or for the credits you spent.

- **A plan.** The {{withdrawalDays}} days start on the day you sign up, which is the day the [free trial](/cloud/trial) starts if the plan has one. They include the trial, so if you withdraw after the first charge and before they end, that charge is refunded in full.
- **A switch to a yearly plan** starts {{withdrawalDays}} days of its own for that payment.
- **A credit pack** starts {{withdrawalDays}} days of its own on the day you buy it.

To withdraw, email [support@postqueen.ai](mailto:support@postqueen.ai) before the days run out and say clearly that you withdraw. Section 10 of the Terms has a model form you can use, but you do not have to. The refund goes to the payment method you used. The plan, or the pack's credits, end when PostQueen confirms the withdrawal.

Business customers have no right of withdrawal.

## Ask for a refund

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the invoice">
    On **Billing**, **Billing history** lists each charge with its invoice number. Note the number of the charge you want back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write to support">
    Email [support@postqueen.ai](mailto:support@postqueen.ai) from the email address on the account, or use the [contact page](https://postqueen.ai/contact). Include the invoice number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the money">
    The refund goes back to the card or payment method you paid with, through Stripe. Banks usually show it within 5 to 10 business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What a refund ends

- **Your plan.** A full refund of the payment for the current period ends the plan it paid for at once. The notice reads "Your PostQueen payment was refunded, so the plan it paid for has ended." Your channels and posts stay, and you can subscribe again from Billing at any time.
- **A credit pack.** A refunded pack loses the credits you have not used.
- **The trial is free.** The $1 authorization placed when the [free trial](/cloud/trial) starts is released at once and never charged, so there is nothing to refund.
- **Canceling is separate.** Canceling stops the next renewal and does not refund the current period. [Manage your subscription](/cloud/subscription#cancel) shows how to cancel.

## Deleting your account

Deleting your account cancels the plans of every workspace you own at once, then deletes your account, workspaces, channels and posts. It does not refund anything by itself, so ask for a refund first. [Settings](/using/settings#delete-your-account) has the steps.

What is removed and when, and the records the law requires PostQueen to keep, are on [Delete my account](https://postqueen.ai/delete-my-account).
