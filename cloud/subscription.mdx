**Where:** **Billing**, from the **Upgrade** row at the bottom of the left rail (**Billing & invoices** on Ultimate, **Founding member** for founding members), or **Billing & invoices** in the account menu.

Only a workspace Admin or Super Admin can open Billing. Other members see **Billing is managed by admins** and need to ask one of them.

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the billing period">
    Use the **Monthly** and **Yearly** switch above the plan cards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the new plan">
    On its card, choose **Upgrade to** or **Downgrade to** followed by the plan name. On your current plan's card, **Switch to yearly** or **Switch to monthly** changes only the period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm what you lose">
    Moving from a plan with team members to Creator asks you to confirm that your team members will be removed from the workspace (switched off, not deleted). Choose **Yes, continue** to go ahead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the change takes effect depends on what the new plan costs a month. A yearly price counts as a twelfth of itself.

- **A plan that costs more a month** is charged now: the prorated difference is billed right away, and the new plan applies once that payment goes through. You see "Subscription updated successfully". If the card is declined, you keep your current plan and PostQueen asks you to update the payment method. The rest of the current period gets the new plan's extra credits, in proportion to the time left.
- **A plan that costs less a month, or a move from yearly to monthly,** waits for renewal. Nothing is charged and there is no credit for the unused time. You keep your current plan until the renewal date, and the new plan starts then. The plan card reads **Changes on** and the date, and a message confirms that you keep your current plan until then. Because a yearly plan costs less a month, moving from monthly to yearly on the same plan also starts at renewal.
- **During the free trial**, nothing has been paid yet, so any change applies at once.

### Yearly plans and the consent box

A yearly plan adds a full year of credits as soon as the year is paid. To choose yearly, tick the box above the plans (in **Payment details** when you first subscribe). It reads: "I want my yearly credits right away, and I understand I lose my 14-day right to withdraw once I use them." Without it, PostQueen asks you to tick the box before it continues with yearly.

### What a smaller plan switches off

When the smaller plan starts:


- **Channels** above the new limit are disabled, newest first. They are not deleted. A post that comes due on a disabled channel fails with "Channel disabled", so reschedule it after you turn the channel back on.
- **Team members** are switched off when the new plan has none. Super Admins, such as the workspace owner, keep access. Upgrading again switches the members back on.
- **Upgrading again** turns the channels the downgrade disabled back on, oldest first, up to the new limit. Channels you disabled yourself stay off.

## Buy more credits

**Credits**, on Billing, shows your balance and when the next of it expires. Under **Buy more credits** there are three packs:

| Pack | Price |
|---|---|
| 100 credits | $10 |
| 500 credits | $45 |
| 1,000 credits | $80 |

Tick the box under the packs, choose **Buy**, and pay on the Stripe checkout page. The box reads: "I want these credits right away, and I understand I lose my 14-day right to withdraw once I use them." Back on Billing, "Payment received. Your credits will show up in a moment." confirms it.

Packs top up a plan, so the card shows once the workspace has one. Bought credits last 12 months. The credits closest to running out are spent first, so a plan's monthly credits go before a pack's. What credits pay for is on [Credits](/cloud/credits).

## Update your card and get invoices

Under **Payment method & invoices**, choose **Open billing portal**. The portal is where you change the card, add a tax ID and download invoices and receipts.

### Billing history

**Billing history**, lower on the Billing page, lists your invoices without leaving PostQueen, newest first. Each row shows what was billed (the plan and **Monthly** or **Yearly**, a credits pack, or **Founding member**), the date, the invoice number, the amount and its status: **Paid**, **Pending**, **Failed** or **Void**. The two buttons on a row are **View invoice** and **Download invoice**, a PDF.

Six invoices show at first. **Show older invoices** adds six more. Before the first payment the list reads "No invoices yet. They show up here after your first payment." Like the rest of Billing, only a workspace Admin or Super Admin sees it.

## Cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the cancellation">
    Choose **Cancel subscription** next to **Open billing portal**, then **Yes, cancel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Consider the offer">
    On a monthly plan, you may be offered 50% off for 3 months instead. Choose **Apply 50% discount for 3 months** to keep the plan at that price, or **Cancel my subscription** to go on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a reason">
    Write at least 20 characters of feedback, then choose **Cancel Subscription**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The plan keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, and nothing more is charged. Billing shows the end date and a **Reactivate subscription** button. Choose it before that date to keep the plan as it was.

Two cases end at once instead:

- **The last payment failed.** Canceling ends the plan immediately.
- **A founding-member trial.** Canceling returns the workspace to no plan immediately, and you lose the lifetime offer.

Deleting your account also cancels the plans of the workspaces you own. See [Refunds](/cloud/refunds#deleting-your-account).

## When a payment fails

If Stripe cannot charge a renewal, you get an email and an in-app notice, and Billing shows "We could not charge your credit card" with **Update payment method**. The plan keeps working while Stripe retries the payment. The next attempt uses the card you set in the billing portal. If the payment still cannot be collected, the plan ends.

## When a payment is disputed

If a payment to PostQueen is disputed with your bank (a chargeback) and the workspace has no subscription still running, such as a founding-member payment, the plan is suspended at once. Every member gets an email titled "Payment disputed", and the bell shows the same words: "A payment for PostQueen was disputed with your bank, so your plan has been suspended. Contact support if this was not you." **Go to billing** on the notice opens Billing.

The workspace is then without a plan, as described below. If you did not dispute the payment, email [support@postqueen.ai](mailto:support@postqueen.ai). A dispute over a past payment does not suspend a subscription that is still running, and a bank's query that takes no money back does not suspend anything.

## When a plan ends

The workspace has no plan. Its channels are switched off, not deleted, and scheduled posts stop publishing. Auto Post feeds are switched off and stay off until you turn them on again. Your posts, channels and settings stay until you delete them. To come back, choose a plan on Billing. A workspace gets one free trial, so coming back starts the paid plan at once.

## Coupons and the founding-member offer

- **Coupons** go in **Have a discount coupon?** at checkout.
- **Founding member** is a one-time payment of $99 that keeps the workspace on Pro for good, with no renewal. It is open for 24 hours after you sign up and during the free trial. On Billing, choose **Switch to lifetime**. Bought during the trial, it is charged when the trial ends.
