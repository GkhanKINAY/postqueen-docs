**Billing** (`/billing`) is where admins change plan. It is on the left rail and in the user menu as **Billing & invoices**. It is not a Settings tab.

## Change plan

Pick a tier on that page. Stripe prorates. The same page starts checkout for a new subscription.

## Card and invoices

**Open billing portal** / **Payment method & invoices** creates a Stripe Customer Portal session and returns you to `/billing`. Portal options beyond that are Stripe Dashboard configuration, not extra PostQueen screens.

## Cancel

**Cancel** calls `POST /billing/cancel`. A normal paid subscription sets `cancel_at_period_end`, so you keep access until the period ends. Calling cancel again while that is scheduled **reactivates** the subscription.

A founding deal that is still in its trail can revoke immediately to FREE instead of waiting for the period end.

## Who can press the buttons

Billing actions need an organisation admin. Everyone else sees Billing as someone else's job.
