PostQueen Cloud is the hosted product at [app.postqueen.ai](https://app.postqueen.ai). Same calendar as a self-hosted install. Billing runs through Stripe. You manage it at **Billing** (`/billing`) in the left rail, or **Billing & invoices** in the user menu. Admins only.

Four sellable plans: Creator, Growth, Pro, Ultimate. What each one unlocks: [Plans](/cloud/plans) and the limit table in [Plans and limits](/using/plans-and-limits). What it costs on the marketing site can move; live checkout is the Billing page.

New organisations eligible for a trial get **7 days**. [Trial](/cloud/trial).

Cancel a normal paid subscription and access continues until the period ends. [Subscription](/cloud/subscription). Card and invoices: **Open billing portal** on that same page (Stripe Customer Portal, then back to `/billing`).

When you hit a cap, the app says so in plain words. [Limits](/cloud/limits). Moving down a plan disables extra channels; it does not delete posts. [Downgrade](/cloud/downgrade).

Coupons at checkout, a founding lifetime deal into Pro, and an Affiliate link in the user menu when it is configured: [Codes and offers](/cloud/codes-and-offers).

There is no self-serve refund button on Billing. Billing trouble: [Billing troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/billing). A person: [Support](/support).
