She is already running at [app.postqueen.ai](https://app.postqueen.ai). Same calendar as a self-hosted install. We keep the servers. Stripe takes the card.

New workspaces get **seven days free**. After that, four plans: Creator, Growth, Pro and Ultimate.

Open **Billing** in the left rail, or **Billing & invoices** in the user menu. Admins only. That is where you start the trial, pick a plan, and open the Stripe portal for the card and invoices. What it costs today: [postqueen.ai/pricing](https://postqueen.ai/pricing).

There is no refund button on Billing, but a charge is refunded in full if you ask within 30 days of it, on a monthly or a yearly plan: [Refunds](/cloud/subscription#refunds). Charge problems: [Billing troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/billing).
