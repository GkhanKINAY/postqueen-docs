The numbers: [Plans and limits](/using/plans-and-limits). This page is what Cloud shows when you meet one.

| Quota | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Channels (connect) | *You have reached the maximum number of channels for your subscription. Please upgrade your subscription to add more channels.* |
| Channels (enable toggle) | *You have reached the maximum number of channels* |
| Posts this period | *…maximum number of posts for your subscription…* |
| Webhooks | *Your plan is at its webhook limit. Remove one or upgrade to add another.* |
| AI images | Toast: *You are out of AI credits for this month.* Picture editor **AI Img**: opens Billing, *Click to purchase more credits* |
| AI videos | *…maximum number of generated videos…* |
| Team / Auto Post / AI flag | *…subscription does not include…* and an upgrade prompt |

The post counter uses your subscription anniversary, not the calendar month. Drafts do not count until you schedule them.

Disable a channel to free a slot without deleting it: [Channel limits](/troubleshooting/channel-limits).
