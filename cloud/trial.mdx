**Where:** the plan picker that opens right after you sign up. Later, **Billing**, from the **Upgrade** row at the bottom of the left rail.

Every new workspace gets one {{trialDays}}-day free trial on the plan you choose, with that plan's channels, AI image and video credits and features. Checkout asks for a card and charges $0 today.

## Start the trial

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.postqueen.ai](https://app.postqueen.ai). The [Quickstart](/quickstart) walks through it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Under **Choose a Plan**, pick Creator, Growth, Pro or Ultimate, and **Monthly** or **Yearly**. [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans) compares them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your card">
    Enter the card and your billing address. If you have a coupon, enter it under **Have a discount coupon?** The order summary shows $0.00 under **Due today**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the trial">
    Choose **Pay $0 Today - Start your free trial!** The trial starts and the app opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens to your card

- **Today:** \$0. To check the card, PostQueen places a \$1 authorization on it and releases it at once. It is never charged, though your bank may show it as pending for a short time.
- **When the trial ends:** the plan starts and the card is charged the plan's monthly or yearly price, plus tax where it applies. **Billing** shows the amount while the trial runs.
- **If you cancel first:** nothing is charged. See [Cancel before the trial ends](#cancel-before-the-trial-ends).

## What waits for the paid plan

<TrialLocks />

In **Add Channel**, X's setup step shows "X unlocks after your trial" in place of its connect button. The AI Copilot page and the **AI Copilot** tab in Create Post show "AI Copilot unlocks after your trial". In the **Generate video** window, **Generate** with **Seedance 2.0 (Audio + Video)** opens a **Trial** dialog instead of making the video, and no credit is used. The API and the MCP server answer `This video is not available in trial mode`.

During the trial, PostQueen also refuses a social account that another PostQueen account connected before. End the trial to add it.

Everything else works from the first day: every other channel you can connect, scheduling, analytics, AI images, AI videos with **Image Text Slides**, Auto Post, webhooks, the MCP server and the public API, and team invites on Growth and up.

## End the trial early

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Choose **Upgrade** at the bottom of the left rail, or **Billing & invoices** in the account menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the trial">
    Choose **End free trial** on the trial banner above the plan cards. There is no second confirmation: PostQueen charges the card for the plan right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    When the payment goes through, you see "Your trial is finished and your card has been charged." X, Seedance videos and the AI Copilot are unlocked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **End free trial to unlock X** and **End free trial to unlock AI Copilot** buttons on the lock cards do the same thing.

## Cancel before the trial ends

Only a workspace Admin or Super Admin can cancel. On **Billing**, choose **Cancel subscription** and follow the steps. The trial keeps running until its last day, then ends without a charge. [Manage your subscription](/cloud/subscription#cancel) shows the whole cancel flow.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| “X unlocks when your free trial ends. End the trial to connect it now.” | End the trial on **Billing**, or wait for it to end, then connect X again. |
| “You are currently on trial, in order to use the feature you must finish the trial” | Shown for a Seedance video. Choose **Finish the trial, charge me now** to start the paid plan, or close the dialog and make the video with **Image Text Slides**. |
| `This video is not available in trial mode` | The API or an agent asked for a Seedance video. End the trial on **Billing**, or use `image-text-slides`. |
| “This social channel was connected previously to another PostQueen account.” | Choose **Fast track - Charge me now** to end the trial and add the channel, or **Cancel**. |
| “We could not charge your credit card” after **End free trial** | Choose **Update payment method**, fix the card in the billing portal, and try again. The trial stays active until the payment succeeds. |
