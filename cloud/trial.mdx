New workspaces that qualify get **seven days**. On Billing the button is **Start 7 days free trial**.

Some networks stay locked until the trial ends. A few video engines say they are not available in trial mode. Stripe may hold a small amount on the card. That is a hold, not the plan charge.

Wait it out and paid billing starts. **End free trial** starts paid billing now. Cancel before it converts and there is no plan charge. You can cancel from Billing without talking to a person.
