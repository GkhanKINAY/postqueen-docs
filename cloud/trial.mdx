Eligible Cloud organisations get a **7-day** trial (`trial_period_days: 7` on Stripe Checkout). The Billing button reads **Start 7 days free trial**.

## While it is running

Some providers stay locked until the trial ends. Hitting one returns you to a finish-trial dialog. Some video engines answer *This video is not available in trial mode*.

Stripe may place a small temporary card authorization. That is a hold, not the plan charge.

## Ending it

| Path | What happens |
|---|---|
| You wait | Subscription leaves `trialing` and paid billing starts |
| **End free trial** | Stripe `trial_end: now` if a trialing subscription exists |
| You cancel before paid period | No plan charge. FAQ on Billing says you can cancel from settings without talking to a person |

A founding lifetime purchase mid-trial can grant Pro now and capture $49 when the trial ends. [Codes and offers](/cloud/codes-and-offers).
