New workspaces that qualify get **seven days**. On Billing the button is **Start 7 days free trial**.

Checkout takes a card, and nothing is due today: it shows $0. When checkout finishes, PostQueen
places a $1 authorization on the card and releases it at once. That is a hold, not the plan charge.

Two things stay locked until the trial ends:

- **X.** Connecting it answers *X unlocks when your free trial ends. End the trial to connect it now.*
- **AI Copilot**, both the chat page and the Copilot inside the composer.

One AI video type also answers that it is not available in trial mode.

Wait it out and paid billing starts. **End free trial** starts paid billing now and unlocks both.
Cancel before it converts and there is no plan charge. You can cancel from Billing without talking
to a person.

Once the plan starts, a charge is refunded in full if you ask within 30 days of it: see
[Refunds](/cloud/subscription#refunds).
