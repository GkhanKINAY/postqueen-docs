## Coupon at checkout

Billing checkout asks **Have a coupon code?** Stripe promotion codes are allowed. Some codes can auto-apply via Stripe metadata.

## Founding lifetime deal

This is **not** a fifth monthly plan. Within 24 hours of registration (or while a trial is still running, for convert), a one-time **$49** charge can grant **Pro**. Routes: `/billing/lifetime`, checkout `POST /billing/lifetime-checkout`, code redeem `POST /billing/lifetime`. The user menu can show **Lifetime deal** when that flag is on.

After that you are on Pro, not on a separate "lifetime" row next to Creator / Growth / Pro / Ultimate.

## Cancel retention

Leaving a paid plan can offer 50% off for three months (`/billing/apply-discount`), or a founding retention of half of $49 (`/billing/apply-lifetime-retention`).

## Affiliate

When `AFFILIATE_URL` is set, **Affiliate** is an external link in the **user menu**. It is not a Settings row and there is no in-app affiliate wallet.
