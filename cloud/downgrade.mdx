Moving to a smaller plan does not delete channels or scheduled posts.

If you have more live channels than the new cap, the newest extras are **disabled**. They keep their history. They will not publish until they are turned back on. Upgrade and they come back on their own, oldest first. Disabling another channel yourself only lets you turn one of them back on by hand.

Dropping to a plan without team members disables every member below superadmin. It does not remove them. Moving to FREE deactivates Auto Post; turn it back on after you subscribe again.

Posts on a disabled channel stay on the calendar, but a post that comes due while its channel is disabled is not sent. It fails with *Channel disabled* and has to be rescheduled.
