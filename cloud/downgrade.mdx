Moving to a smaller plan does not delete channels or scheduled posts.

## Channels

If you have more live channels than the new cap, the newest extras are **disabled** (`autoDisabledAt` is set). They keep their history. They will not publish until you turn them back on.

The app tells you how many were switched off, and that you can upgrade or remove another channel to turn one back on.

Upgrade, or free a slot with **Disable Channel** on something else, and auto-disabled channels come back, oldest disabled first.

## Team

Dropping to a plan without team members **disables** every member below superadmin. It does not remove them.

## Auto Post

Moving to FREE deactivates Auto Post crons. You turn them back on after you subscribe again.

## Posts

There is no code path that deletes scheduled posts on downgrade. Posts on a disabled channel stay on the calendar and will not go out until that channel is enabled.
