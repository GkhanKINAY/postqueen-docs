Four plans: **Creator**, **Growth**, **Pro**, **Ultimate**.

Price lives on [postqueen.ai/pricing](https://postqueen.ai/pricing). Checkout is **Billing**. The numbers the app actually checks are on [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans).

| Plan | Channels | Team |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creator | 5 | No |
| Growth | 10 | Yes |
| Pro | 30 | Yes |
| Ultimate | Unlimited | Yes |

Every paid plan includes the public API, Auto Post, and the picture editor's **AI Img** tab.

A one-time founding deal can grant Pro. That is not a fifth plan: [Codes and offers](/cloud/subscription).
