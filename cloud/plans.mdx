Prices below are the amounts in the billing code (`CREATOR` / `GROWTH` / `PRO` / `AGENCY`). The marketing page at [postqueen.ai/pricing](https://postqueen.ai/pricing) can move. Checkout is **Billing** (`/billing`).

| Plan | API key | Month | Year | Channels | Team | AI images | AI videos | Webhooks |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Creator | `CREATOR` | 20 | 132 | 5 | No | 20 | 3 | 2 |
| Growth | `GROWTH` | 33 | 264 | 10 | Yes | 100 | 10 | 10 |
| Pro | `PRO` | 49 | 396 | 30 | Yes | 300 | 30 | 30 |
| Ultimate | `AGENCY` | 99 | 792 | Unlimited | Yes | 500 | 60 | 10,000 |

Every paid plan includes the public API, Auto Post, and the picture editor's **AI Img** tab (including Creator). Posts per month are stored as 1,000,000; the billing screen prints anything above 10,000 as **Unlimited**. Ultimate's channel cap is the same idiom.

Yearly is cheaper than twelve months (Creator about 45% off monthly; the others about 33%).

Retired keys `STANDARD`, `TEAM` and `ULTIMATE` still resolve for old subscriptions. They are not offered for sale.

A one-time founding deal can grant Pro. That is not a fifth row in this table: [Codes and offers](/cloud/codes-and-offers).
