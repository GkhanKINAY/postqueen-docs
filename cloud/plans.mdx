**Where:** **Billing**, from the **Upgrade** row at the bottom of the left rail, or **Billing & invoices** in the account menu. Only workspace admins see Billing.

PostQueen has four plans, billed monthly or yearly. A new workspace starts with a {{trialDays}}-day free trial on the plan it picks. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax, which checkout adds where it applies.

Switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly** at the top of the plan cards to see both prices.

## What each plan includes

| Plan | Channels | Team members | Credits | Webhooks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creator | 5 | Owner only | 150 credits a month | 2 |
| Growth | 10 | Unlimited | 300 credits a month | 10 |
| Pro | 30 | Unlimited | 500 credits a month | 30 |
| Ultimate | Unlimited | Unlimited | 1,000 credits a month | 10,000 |

Every plan also includes the calendar and the post composer, the AI Copilot, analytics, Auto Post from RSS feeds, Auto-Plugs, Social Sets, signatures, the MCP server for AI agents and the public API. No plan has a monthly post limit, and plans differ only in the columns above.

## Credits

Credits are one balance per workspace. They pay for AI images and AI videos, the AI Copilot and AI agents that use PostQueen's own agent over MCP (by the tokens they use), the other AI writing features, and X, which charges PostQueen for every post published through its API. [Credits](/cloud/credits) lists what each thing costs.

- **Monthly plans** add the plan's credits at the start of every billing month.
- **Yearly plans** add twelve months of credits at once, as soon as the year is paid. Choosing yearly asks you to tick a consent box first: see [Manage your subscription](/cloud/subscription#yearly-plans-and-the-consent-box).
- **The free trial** adds one month of the plan's credits, on a yearly plan too.
- **A gift of 5 credits** is added every calendar month to a paid plan once its trial is over.
- **Packs** of 100, 500 or 1,000 credits can be bought on **Billing** when a plan's credits run low.

Billing shows the balance and when the next credits expire.

## How the limits count

- **Channels.** Every connected channel counts, including disabled ones. A channel that is waiting to be reconnected does not. Disabling a channel does not make room for a new one; it only lets you turn another disabled channel back on.
- **Credits.** A plan's credits do not carry over: the next period's credits replace what is left as soon as they arrive. If a renewal is late, the old ones stay spendable for up to 3 days past the period's end. The monthly gift expires at the end of its month, and bought credits last 12 months. Work that fails gives its credits back. See [Credits](/cloud/credits).
- **Posts.** No plan has a monthly post limit. Each network still applies its own daily limits, and the public API takes 30 requests to create posts an hour per workspace: see [Limits and errors](/public-api/limits-and-errors#rate-limits).
- **Team members.** Creator is for one person, the workspace owner. Growth and up have no seat count and no per-seat price. Moving down to Creator switches off every member except the owner, and moving back up switches them on again.

## When you reach a limit

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| “You have reached the maximum number of channels for your subscription. Please upgrade your subscription to add more channels.” | In the **Payment Required** dialog, choose **Move to billing** and pick a bigger plan, or delete a channel you no longer use. |
| “Your plan is at its webhook limit. Remove one or upgrade to add another.” | Delete a webhook, or upgrade. |
| “Not enough credits: this costs N and the balance is N.” | Shown in the **Payment Required** dialog for an AI image or video, or a post to X. Choose **Move to billing** and buy a credits pack, or move to a plan with more credits. |
| “Your subscription does not include team members. Please upgrade your subscription to invite your team.” | Shown under **Unlock team members** in **Settings > Teams**. Choose **Upgrade plan** and move to Growth or higher. |
| “Only a workspace admin can do this. Please ask an admin of this workspace.” | Ask an Admin or Super Admin of the workspace. |

<Warning>
Deleting a channel also deletes that channel's own posts in PostQueen: scheduled posts, drafts and the records of published ones. Read [Connect a channel](/using/connect-a-channel) before you delete one to free a slot.
</Warning>
