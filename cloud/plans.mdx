**Where:** **Billing**, from the **Upgrade** row at the bottom of the left rail, or **Billing & invoices** in the account menu. Only workspace admins see Billing.

PostQueen has four plans. Each one starts with a {{trialDays}}-day free trial and bills monthly or yearly. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax, which checkout adds where it applies.

## Prices

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Yearly, per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creator | {{priceCreator}} | $132 | $11 (5 months free) |
| Growth | {{priceGrowth}} | $264 | $22 (4 months free) |
| Pro | {{pricePro}} | $396 | $33 (4 months free) |
| Ultimate | {{priceUltimate}} | $792 | $66 (4 months free) |

Switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly** at the top of the plan cards to see both prices.

## What each plan includes

| Plan | Channels | Team members | AI images a month | Webhooks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creator | 5 | Owner only | 20 | 2 |
| Growth | 10 | Unlimited | 100 | 10 |
| Pro | 30 | Unlimited | 300 | 30 |
| Ultimate | Unlimited | Unlimited | 500 | 10,000 |

Every plan also includes the calendar and the post composer, the AI Copilot, analytics, Auto Post from RSS feeds, Auto-Plugs, Social Sets, signatures, the MCP server for AI agents and the public API. No plan has a monthly post limit, and plans differ only in the columns above.

## How the limits count

- **Channels.** Every connected channel counts, including disabled ones. A channel that is waiting to be reconnected does not. Disabling a channel does not make room for a new one; it only lets you turn another disabled channel back on.
- **AI images.** Credits reset every month on the day your subscription started, on yearly plans too. Unused credits do not carry over.
- **Posts.** No plan has a monthly post limit. Each network still applies its own daily limits, and the public API has an hourly posting limit per workspace.
- **Team members.** Creator is for one person, the workspace owner. Growth and up have no seat count and no per-seat price. Moving down to Creator switches off every member except the owner, and moving back up switches them on again.

## When you reach a limit

| Limit | What you see | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Channels | "You have reached the maximum number of channels for your subscription. Please upgrade your subscription to add more channels." in a **Payment Required** dialog | Choose **Move to billing** and pick a bigger plan, or delete a channel you no longer use |
| Webhooks | "Your plan is at its webhook limit. Remove one or upgrade to add another." | Delete a webhook, or upgrade |
| AI images | "You are out of AI credits for this month." | Wait for the monthly reset, or upgrade |
| Team members | **Unlock team members** in **Settings > Teams**, with "Your subscription does not include team members. Please upgrade your subscription to invite your team." | Choose **Upgrade plan** and move to Growth or higher |
| An admin-only action | "Only a workspace admin can do this. Please ask an admin of this workspace." | Ask an Admin or Super Admin of the workspace |

<Warning>
Deleting a channel also deletes that channel's own posts in PostQueen: scheduled posts, drafts and the records of published ones. Read [Connect a channel](/using/connect-a-channel) before you delete one to free a slot.
</Warning>
