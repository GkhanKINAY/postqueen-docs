Most people arrive with the same handful of questions. Autopilot, self-hosting, what she can post
to. Cannot find yours? The [support page](/support) is the fastest route to a human.

## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is PostQueen, in one sentence?" icon="crown">
    PostQueen is an AI social media assistant who writes the copy, designs the visuals and schedules posts across 30+ networks, while you review the calendar.

    New here? The [quickstart](/quickstart) takes you from an empty account to your first scheduled post in about five minutes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should I use the cloud or self-host her?" icon="scale-balanced">
    Both run the same code.

    | | |
    |---|---|
    | **[Cloud](https://postqueen.ai)** | 7-day free trial, nothing to patch |
    | **[Self-host](/installation/overview)** | Free forever, your database and your tokens |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a mobile app?" icon="mobile">
    On the way. Meanwhile [app.postqueen.ai](https://app.postqueen.ai) runs the whole calendar from a phone browser, and she answers on WhatsApp or Telegram through [OpenClaw](/agents/openclaw) or [Hermes](/agents/hermes).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Agents and the API

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can my AI assistant use her?" icon="robot">
    Any assistant that can run a command or call a tool.

    - **[MCP](/mcp/introduction)** gives any client 11 tools, from one URL
    - **[CLI](/cli/introduction)** via `npx skills add GkhanKINAY/postqueen-agent`
    - **[Public API](/public-api/introduction)** for your own scripts

    Compared side by side: [which interface](/which-interface).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there API limits?" icon="gauge-high">
    30 requests per hour on create-post, and nothing else is capped by it. Schedule several posts in one request when you need throughput.

    Self-hosting? Raise or lower it with `API_LIMIT`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
