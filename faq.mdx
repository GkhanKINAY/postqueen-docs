## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does PostQueen do?" icon="calendar-days">
    PostQueen publishes your posts to your social channels at the times you pick, from one calendar. You write a post in the app, or ask an AI agent, run the `postqueen` CLI or call the API, and every post lands on the same calendar.

    The [Quickstart](/quickstart) takes you from sign-up to your first scheduled post.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a card for the free trial?" icon="credit-card">
    Yes. Every new workspace gets a {{trialDays}}-day free trial on the plan you pick. Checkout asks for a card and charges $0 today. To check the card, PostQueen places a $1 authorization and releases it at once.

    Cancel before the trial ends and nothing is charged. [Free trial](/cloud/trial) has the details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is locked during the trial?" icon="lock">
    <TrialLocks />

    Everything else works from day one. [Free trial](/cloud/trial) lists it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free plan?" icon="tag">
    No. After the trial, a workspace needs one of the four paid plans to publish. Plans start at {{priceCreator}} a month: [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a mobile app?" icon="smartphone">
    There is nothing to install. [app.postqueen.ai](https://app.postqueen.ai) works in your phone's browser, with the same calendar and composer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Channels

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which networks can I connect?" icon="share-2">
    PostQueen supports {{networks}} networks. Each one has a status today:

    | Status | Networks |
    |---|---|
    | Available | Bluesky, DEV, Hashnode, Lemmy, Listmonk, Moltbook, Nostr, WordPress |
    | In review | Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, X, YouTube |
    | Soon | LinkedIn, Pinterest, Google Business Profile, Reddit, Mastodon, Tumblr, VK, Farcaster, MeWe, Twitch, Kick, Dribbble, Telegram, Discord, Slack, Skool, Whop |

    [All channels](/channels/overview) has a page for each network.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does In review mean?" icon="hourglass">
    The network is still reviewing PostQueen's app. Until the review passes, the network sets a limit: for example, only some accounts can connect, or posts are published as private. Each network's page states its limit in one sentence at the top.

    X is the exception: it connects once your paid plan starts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can I not connect a network marked Soon?" icon="clock">
    **Add Channel** lists every network, but the ones marked Soon cannot be connected on PostQueen yet. Their pages say what PostQueen will post there once they can.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many channels can I connect?" icon="plug">
    It depends on your plan: [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans) has the numbers. Disabled channels count toward the limit, so disabling a channel does not make room for a new one. Delete a channel, or move to a bigger plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my posts when I delete a channel?" icon="trash-2">
    PostQueen deletes that channel's posts in PostQueen: scheduled posts, drafts and the records of published posts. Scheduled posts on it will not go out. Copies of a post on your other channels stay, and posts that are already live stay on the network.

    Reconnecting the channel later does not bring the posts back. [Connect a channel](/using/connect-a-channel#disable-or-delete-a-channel) has the steps.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Posting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to approve each post before it goes out?" icon="circle-check">
    No. A scheduled post publishes at its time without asking again. To hold a post back, save it as a draft: a draft keeps its date on the calendar and does not publish until you schedule it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change a post after I schedule it?" icon="square-pen">
    Yes, until it is published. Open it from the [calendar](/using/calendar) to edit it, or drag it to another time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a limit on how many posts I can schedule?" icon="infinity">
    No plan has a monthly post limit. Each network still applies its own daily limits, and the public API has an hourly posting limit per workspace: [Limits and errors](/public-api/limits-and-errors).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A post did not go out. What now?" icon="triangle-alert">
    A failed post shows a red mark on the calendar, and the reason is in its tooltip and in your notifications. [A post did not go out](/troubleshooting/post-failed) explains each reason and how to send the post again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## AI agents and the API

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can my AI agent post for me?" icon="bot">
    Yes. Any agent that supports MCP can connect to PostQueen and use its {{mcpToolsKey}} tools with your API key. A sign-in address without a key offers {{mcpToolsOauth}} tools and is not tested yet. Agents that run commands can use the `postqueen` CLI instead.

    [Connect your agent](/agents/overview) has a page for each agent.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where is my API key?" icon="key-round">
    <ApiKeyLocation />
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can an agent delete my posts?" icon="shield-check">
    Not over MCP: no MCP tool deletes a post. The CLI and the API can delete posts, because they act with your API key. Give the key only to tools you trust, and rotate it if it leaks.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?" icon="circle-x">
    On **Billing**, choose **Cancel subscription**. Only a workspace Admin or Super Admin can cancel. The plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for: [Manage your subscription](/cloud/subscription#cancel).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?" icon="receipt">
    Yes. Any charge is refunded in full when you ask within {{refundDays}} days of it. Email [support@postqueen.ai](mailto:support@postqueen.ai): [Refunds](/cloud/refunds).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when my plan ends?" icon="calendar-x">
    Your channels are switched off, not deleted, and scheduled posts stop publishing. Your posts and settings stay. Choose a plan on **Billing** to carry on: [When a plan ends](/cloud/subscription#when-a-plan-ends).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Your account and data

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?" icon="user-x">
    In **Settings > Account**, choose **Delete Account** and confirm with your email and password: [Settings](/using/settings#delete-your-account). Your plan is canceled at once, and your data is erased within 30 days.

    [Security and privacy](/security-and-privacy#when-you-delete-your-account) says what is removed and what the law requires PostQueen to keep.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does PostQueen sell my data?" icon="eye-off">
    No. PostQueen uses what it receives from your channels only to publish your posts and show you their results. The [Privacy Policy](https://postqueen.ai/privacy-policy) lists every kind of data it holds and why.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
