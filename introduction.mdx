## Why PostQueen?

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="She writes it" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Give her a rough idea and she turns it into a post, shaped for each network you picked
  </Card>
  <Card title="One calendar, 30+ networks" icon="calendar">
    X, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Discord, your WordPress blog. One week on one screen
  </Card>
  <Card title="She designs it too" icon="image">
    AI images and video from a prompt, plus a built-in design editor, on plans that include AI
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reach her from anywhere" icon="robot">
    Your terminal, a chat with Claude or ChatGPT, or the workflow tool your team already runs
  </Card>
  <Card title="You stay in control" icon="eye">
    Everything is on the calendar before it publishes, so you can change it, move it or drop it
  </Card>
  <Card title="Yours to host" icon="server">
    AGPL-3.0. Run her on your own server and your tokens never leave your database
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Reach her from anywhere

Most of the time you will work in the app. She answers from everywhere else too, and all of these
ride the same public API, so a post lands on the same calendar whichever door you came through.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Agents" icon="robot" href="/agents/overview">
    Let Claude, ChatGPT or any assistant run your calendar
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automation" icon="bolt" href="/automation/overview">
    n8n, Zapier and Make put posts on her calendar, and an RSS feed fills it on its own
  </Card>
  <Card title="CLI" icon="terminal" href="/cli/introduction">
    Script posts, media and analytics from any shell, with clean JSON every time
  </Card>
  <Card title="MCP" icon="network-wired" href="/mcp/introduction">
    One URL, and Claude Code or ChatGPT can post for you. Nothing to install
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public API" icon="code" href="/public-api/introduction">
    Schedule posts, push media and read the numbers over REST
  </Card>
  <Card title="Self-hosting" icon="server" href="/installation/overview">
    Run the whole thing yourself, on your own hardware
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
