## What is PostQueen?

PostQueen is a social media management platform with an AI assistant at the center of it. You describe a post in one sentence, and she writes the copy for each channel, makes the image or video that goes with it and puts the result on your calendar.

She posts to 30+ networks and runs on PostQueen Cloud or on your own server, from the same open-source code. Most people work with her in the app. She also answers from outside it, so an AI assistant, a shell script or a workflow tool can fill that same calendar.

She runs on your terms. A scheduled post goes out at the time you set without anyone touching it, and anything saved as a draft sits there until you publish it.

## What she does

- **Scheduling.** One calendar for every channel you have connected. A post that fails on the network's side is retried instead of dropped.
- **Writing and design.** She shapes the copy for each platform and generates the image or the video, voiceover included.
- **Analytics.** Numbers per platform and per post, for the networks that report them back.

## Reach her from outside the app

The app is not the only way in. She also answers from a terminal, from a chat with an AI assistant and from the tools your team already runs.

| If this is you | Start with |
|---|---|
| You would rather ask for a post in plain language than write the call | [Agents](/agents/overview) or the [MCP server](/mcp/introduction) |
| You are already in a terminal, a shell script or a CI job | [CLI](/cli/introduction) |
| Your trigger already runs in n8n, Zapier or Make | [Automation](/automation/overview) |
| You are writing the code yourself, or you need something the others do not carry | [Public API](/public-api/introduction) |

All of them sit on the same public API, so a post lands on the same calendar whichever route you take. [Which interface](/which-interface) compares them in detail, down to what each one can and cannot reach.
