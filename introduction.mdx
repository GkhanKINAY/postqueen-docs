PostQueen is an AI social media manager. Tell her what to share and she writes it, designs it and puts it on your calendar.

These docs are the how: connecting your channels, running her from a terminal or a chat, hosting her yourself if that is your thing.

## Reach her from anywhere

Most of the time you will work in the app. She answers from everywhere else too: your terminal, a chat with an assistant, the workflow tool your team already runs.

| If this is you | Start with |
|---|---|
| You would rather ask for a post than write the call for it | [Agents](/agents/overview) or the [MCP server](/mcp/introduction) |
| You live in a terminal, and a script or a CI job does the asking | [CLI](/cli/introduction) |
| Something else fires first: a sheet row, a new article, a Monday timer | [Automation](/automation/overview) |
| You are writing the code yourself, or you need something the others do not carry | [Public API](/public-api/introduction) |

All four ride the same public API, so a post lands on the same calendar whichever door you came through. [Which interface](/which-interface) sets them side by side, down to what each one can and cannot reach.
