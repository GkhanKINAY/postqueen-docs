**Where:** by email to [support@postqueen.ai](mailto:support@postqueen.ai). A person reads every message.

Write from the email address on your PostQueen account, so support can find it without asking. For a bug you are happy to describe in public, GitHub Issues works too.

## What to put in your message

The first reply can solve the problem when it has these details.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In the app">
    | Detail | Example |
    |---|---|
    | What you did, step by step | "In Create Post I picked my Bluesky channel and chose **Schedule**." |
    | What you expected | "The post to appear on Friday at 9:00." |
    | What happened instead, with the exact message | A screenshot, or the message copied word for word. |
    | The channel and the time | The network, the account name, and the time with your time zone. |
    | The workspace | Its name, when your account belongs to more than one. |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="The API, the CLI or an agent">
    | Detail | Example |
    |---|---|
    | The request or the command | The endpoint and body, or the full `postqueen` command. Write `YOUR_API_KEY` where the key was. |
    | The answer | The status code and the response body, or the tool's reply in your agent. |
    | The time | With your time zone, so support can find the request. |
    | The version | For the CLI, the output of `postqueen --version`. For an agent, its name and how it connects. |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
Never send your API key, a password, a sign-in code or a card number, by email or on GitHub. Support never needs them. If a key was shared by mistake, an Admin can replace it with **Rotate key** under **Connections > API Keys**, and the old key stops working.
</Warning>

## Billing, refunds and your account

Email support from the address on the account for any of these. Most can also be done in the app.

| You want to | Do it yourself | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Get a refund | Email support within {{refundDays}} days of the charge. Any charge is refunded in full. | [Refunds](/cloud/refunds) |
| Cancel your plan | **Billing > Cancel subscription**, as an Admin or Super Admin. | [Manage your subscription](/cloud/subscription#cancel) |
| Change the card or get an invoice | **Billing > Open billing portal**. | [Manage your subscription](/cloud/subscription) |
| Delete your account | **Settings > Account > Delete Account**. | [Settings](/using/settings#delete-your-account) |
| Get a copy of your data | Email support and ask for it. | [Security and privacy](/security-and-privacy#your-data) |

If you cannot sign in at all, email support from the address on the account and say what happens when you try.

## A security concern

Found something that looks like a security problem, or think someone else has access to your account? Email [support@postqueen.ai](mailto:support@postqueen.ai) with what you saw and when. Do not open a public GitHub issue for it. [Security and privacy](/security-and-privacy) explains how PostQueen protects your account and your channels.
